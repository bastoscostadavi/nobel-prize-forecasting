My best guess is **Yakir Aharonov and Michael Berry**, for their foundational discoveries concerning **quantum phases**—the Aharonov–Bohm effect and Berry phase. These showed that electromagnetic potentials and the geometry of a system’s evolution can have measurable quantum effects. Their influence spans condensed-matter physics, optics, and quantum computing.

My runner-up would be **Pablo Jarillo-Herrero, Allan MacDonald, and Rafi Bistritzer**, for predicting and demonstrating remarkable electronic behavior in **magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene**.
