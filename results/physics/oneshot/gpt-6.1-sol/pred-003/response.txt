My pick is **Charles Kane, Eugene Mele, and Laurens Molenkamp**, for the prediction and experimental discovery of **topological insulators**—materials that insulate internally but conduct along their surfaces or edges.

Kane and Mele established key theoretical foundations; Molenkamp’s group provided a landmark experimental demonstration. The work revealed a new way to classify electronic matter and has had nearly two decades to demonstrate its importance—a plausible Nobel profile.

A strong alternative is **Michael Berry and Yakir Aharonov**, for discoveries concerning quantum phases.
