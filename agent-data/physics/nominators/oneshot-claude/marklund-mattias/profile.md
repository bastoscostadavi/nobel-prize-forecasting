## Research footprint

Marklund's early work was on nonlinear plasma physics, relativistic plasmas and quantum plasmas. He wrote a *Reviews of Modern Physics* article with P. K. Shukla on nonlinear collective effects in photon–photon and photon–plasma interactions (2006). His main current topic is particle dynamics and radiation in extreme electromagnetic fields: radiation reaction, Breit–Wheeler pair production and the approach to critical (Schwinger-scale) fields. This work was reviewed in "Charged particle motion and radiation in strong electromagnetic fields" (Gonoskov, Blackburn, Marklund & Bulanov, *RMP* 2022).

His recent co-authored work (GU staff list) includes:
- the experimental "Observation of quantum effects on radiation reaction in strong fields" (*Nature Communications* 2026);
- vacuum laser acceleration (*Communications Physics* 2024);
- ion acceleration including radiation reaction and pair production (*HPLSE* 2024);
- data-driven modelling of laser-plasma X-ray sources (*PRAB* 2025).

He combines analytical theory with large-scale particle-in-cell simulation and increasingly uses machine learning.
