My best guess is **Michael Berry and Yakir Aharonov**, for their discoveries about quantum phase—the way a particle’s wave changes as it moves through a physical setup.

Aharonov’s work showed that electromagnetic potentials can affect quantum interference even where the particle encounters no magnetic field. Berry discovered an additional phase arising from the geometry of a system’s evolution. Both contributions are fundamental, experimentally established, and influential across modern physics—a strong fit for a Nobel Prize.
