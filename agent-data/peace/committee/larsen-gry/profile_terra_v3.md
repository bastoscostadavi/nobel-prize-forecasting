## Research and public-policy record

No academic publications were identified.

**Sustainability:** Grieg Group sustainability role and WWF Norway board.

**Middle East:** as AUF leader she backed a consumer boycott of Israeli goods. In January 2006, as Støre's adviser, she said she stood "one hundred percent behind the government's policy… that Israeli goods should not be boycotted" (Aftenbladet, 9 Jan 2006). Al Jazeera (2025) reports that a pro-Israel group has cited her AUF-era position.

**Humanitarian and development work:** as head of CARE Norway she campaigned on women's rights and gender equality, sexual and reproductive health, and the effects of aid cuts.

That period was marked by Norway's peace-and-reconciliation diplomacy, development policy and multilateralism. Her specific portfolio responsibilities as state secretary were not verified.

## Biographical record

She was Secretary General of CARE Norway, an organisation focused on poor women's rights (2015–2020). She then joined the Grieg Group as head of sustainability and public affairs and later managed its holding company. She was on the boards of Grieg Foundation and WWF Norway and was vice chair of Oppsal Fotball.

Born 7 November 1975; grew up in Jevnaker. She studied Norwegian, international politics and history. She led the Workers' Youth League (AUF), Labour's youth wing, from 2002 to 2006, and was a deputy Storting representative for Oppland (1997–2001, 2005–09). In the second Stoltenberg government she was political adviser to Foreign Minister Jonas Gahr Støre (2005–09) and then State Secretary in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (2009–13).
