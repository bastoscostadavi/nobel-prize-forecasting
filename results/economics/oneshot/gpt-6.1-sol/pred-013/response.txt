My prediction: **Steven Berry, James Levinsohn, and Ariel Pakes**, for **developing methods to measure demand, competition, and market power in markets with differentiated products**.

Their “BLP” framework lets economists use market data to estimate how consumers choose among competing products and how firms set prices. It has become a cornerstone of empirical industrial organization, with applications to merger analysis, antitrust, and markets such as automobiles.

It is a foundational methodological contribution with substantial policy impact and a natural three-person attribution—strong ingredients for a Nobel citation. This is a speculative pick; my confidence in any single lineup is low.
