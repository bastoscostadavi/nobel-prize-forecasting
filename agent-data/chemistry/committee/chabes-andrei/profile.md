## Biography

Andrei Chabes is Professor of Medical Chemistry/Biochemistry at Umeå University and Director of the Wallenberg Centre for Molecular Medicine there. His early publications (around 2000) are from Umeå on yeast and mouse ribonucleotide reductase, in the circle of Lars Thelander. He was a postdoc in Bruce Stillman's laboratory at Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory (2002-2004), funded by the Swedish Cancer Society (per search summary of his LinkedIn/Scholar). He is a member of the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences in the Class for biological sciences, so he is a biologist-classified academician sitting on the chemistry committee.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- Core question: how cells regulate the supply of the four dNTPs, and how imbalanced pools affect replication fidelity, mutagenesis, and genome stability.
- Ribonucleotide reductase (RNR) structure and regulation, e.g. the heterodimeric iron-radical subunit of yeast RNR (PNAS 2000).
- Interplay between the DNA-damage/genome-integrity checkpoint and dNTP levels; e.g. "dNTP concentrations do not increase in mammalian cells in response to DNA damage" (2022).
- Ribonucleotide incorporation into DNA as a source of genome instability (Nature Chemical Biology, 2010) and in mitochondrial DNA.
- Disease links: altered dNTP pools accelerate tumour formation in mice (NAR 2024); RRM2B deficiency (PNAS 2025); aging and cancer.
- Methods: yeast and mouse genetics, analytical biochemistry (HPLC and LC-MS/MS quantification of nucleotide pools).
- OpenAlex (Sept 2026): about 110 works, about 7,200 citations, h-index 44.
