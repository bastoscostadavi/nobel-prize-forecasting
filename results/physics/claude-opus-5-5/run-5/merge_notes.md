# Merge notes

## Ambiguous merge and split decisions

- `bloch-immanuel.json` bundles trapped-ion quantum information processing with neutral-atom and optical-lattice quantum simulation. It was retained as a compound one-off candidate because neither narrower group preserves the full nomination.
- `fang-zhong.json` and `samuelson-lars.json` genuinely bundle the quantum spin Hall and quantum anomalous Hall effects. They were retained together as a compound candidate, separate from the narrower topological-insulator and quantum-spin-Hall group.
- `grangier-philippe.json`, `lu-chaoyang.json`, and `zeilinger-anton.json` were merged because the global satellite-network nomination explicitly presents deployment as the realization of the same quantum-key-distribution invention. Jian-Wei Pan is preserved in `other_names`.
- `bergholtz-emil.json` and `stern-ady.json` were merged as the fractional quantum anomalous Hall effect despite referring to different moiré-material realizations of the zero-field fractional Chern-insulator phenomenon.
- `hansson-thors-hans.json` and `viefers-susanne.json` were merged because their collider and interferometer experiments are complementary demonstrations of anyonic braiding statistics in the fractional quantum Hall effect.
- `flensberg-karsten.json` and `simmons-michelle.json` were merged because both nominate the proposal and experimental realization of coherent electron-spin qubits in semiconductor quantum dots.
- The three quantum-optomechanics nominations were merged despite different third nominees because they concern the same achievement of ground-state preparation and quantum control of engineered mechanical motion.
- `abbamonte-peter.json` was retained as a compound one-off candidate because it deliberately joins momentum-space ARPES and real-space spectroscopic-imaging STM, and no other source in this run preserves that full bundle.
