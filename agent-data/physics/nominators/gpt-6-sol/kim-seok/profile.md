## Research footprint

Kim works on the exact, non-perturbative structure of supersymmetric quantum field theories and its use in holography. His single-author 2009 paper computing the complete superconformal index of N=6 Chern–Simons (ABJM) theory is among his most cited. He contributed to supersymmetric Kaluza–Klein truncations (with Gauntlett, Varela, Waldram, 2009). With Hee-Cheol Kim, Joonho Kim and others he used localization and instanton counting on the 5-sphere to study M5-branes and 5d/6d superconformal field theories (2012–2015), and he co-authored the 2016 review "Localization techniques in quantum field theories". Since 2018 his main theme has been microscopic counting of supersymmetric AdS black holes from field theory: "Large AdS black holes from QFT" (2018), a 4d N=1 Cardy formula (2019), "finite N black hole cohomologies" (2023), and 2025–26 work on "fortuity", BPS phases in higher-spin holography, and "quantum black hole cohomologies". He has also studied endpoints of superradiant instabilities in AdS ("grey galaxies" and "dual dressed black holes", with Shiraz Minwalla and others). Across his career the focus moved from computing protected quantities toward using them to understand black hole entropy.
