## Biography

According to the Blusson QMI page, Damascelli received his PhD from the University of Groningen (1994–1999). He was then a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University (1999–2002). He joined UBC as Assistant Professor in 2002, became Associate Professor in 2007, and has been full Professor since 2013. He was Scientific Director of UBC's Stewart Blusson Quantum Matter Institute from 2015 to 2025. In that period the institute grew around a large Canada First Research Excellence Fund-backed program in quantum materials (the institutional growth is inferred from the QMI site's scale; the funding details were not verified here). ## Research footprint

Damascelli is best known for photoemission spectroscopy of correlated electron systems. His review with Z. Hussain and Z.-X. Shen, "Angle-resolved photoemission studies of the cuprate superconductors" (*Rev. Mod. Phys.* 75, 473, 2003), is a standard reference with more than 3,400 citations in Crossref. At UBC his group has extended ARPES into time-resolved and spin-resolved variants and added resonant x-ray scattering (RXS). This work used the UBC-Moore Centre for Ultrafast Quantum Matter and the Quantum Materials Spectroscopy Centre at the Canadian Light Source. Current topics listed by QMI include:
- non-equilibrium dynamics and coherent optical control of quantum materials
- spin-orbit coupling and unconventional superconductivity
- surface engineering by in-situ adatom deposition and strain
- 2D van der Waals materials and oxide heterostructures

Representative recent work:
- a direct time-domain determination of mode-projected electron-phonon coupling (Na et al., *Science* 366, 2019)
- ARPES of a Mott insulator driven through a current-induced insulator-to-metal transition (Suen et al., *Nat. Phys.* 20, 2024)
- a review of time-resolved ARPES (Boschini, Zonno, Damascelli, *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 96, 015003, 2024)
