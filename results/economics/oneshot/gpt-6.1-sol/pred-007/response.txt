My prediction is **Steven Berry, James Levinsohn, and Ariel Pakes**, for **developing methods to measure demand and competition in markets with differentiated products**.

Their influential “BLP” framework lets economists estimate how consumers choose among competing products and how firms set prices. It provides practical tools for assessing market power, mergers, and consumer welfare.

The case for the prize is the combination of methodological innovation, widespread academic adoption, and direct use in competition policy. This is a speculative pick, but it is my strongest candidate.
