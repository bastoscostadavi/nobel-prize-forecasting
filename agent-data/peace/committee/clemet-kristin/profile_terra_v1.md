## Biography

Born 20 April 1957 in Harstad; daughter of former Høyre general secretary Fridtjov Clemet. She qualified as a siviløkonom at NHH (1981). She was personal secretary in the Ministry of Industry (1981–83) and adviser to Prime Minister Kåre Willoch (1985–86). She was Storting member for Oslo (1989–93) and Minister of Labour and Government Administration (1989–90). She edited Høyre's journal *Tidens Tegn* (1993–97) and was deputy director general of NHO, the Confederation of Norwegian Enterprise (1997–2001). As Minister of Education and Research (2001–05) she implemented the Quality Reform in higher education, began the Kunnskapsløftet school reform and passed the independent-schools act. She has led Civita since 2006. Honours: Commander of the Order of St. Olav (2004), "European of the Year" from Europeisk Ungdom (2008), and the Fritt Ord honour (2017, per SNL).

## Research / intellectual footprint

- **Liberal democracy and rule of law:** at Civita she is a leading Norwegian voice for classical liberalism, market economy, individual rights, privacy (*Til forsvar for personvernet*, 2010) and a liberal approach to immigration and integration (*De nye seierherrene*, 2012).
- **European recognition and commentary:** she was named "European of the Year" by the pro-EU youth movement. She has long written critically about populism and "post-factual democracy" (Civita/Aftenposten, 2016).
- **Human rights networks:** council member of the Norwegian Helsinki Committee and board member of Oslo Freedom Forum Norway (2026 register). Former chair of Plan Norway.
- **Knowledge and education policy:** research and higher education reforms; board member of the University of Oslo (to mid-2025).
- No specialist writing on arms control was found. Her foreign-policy views come mainly from columns about democracy, liberal order and authoritarianism.

## Attributed public statements

- On Trump (2025, quoted by Al Jazeera): after "just over 100 days as president, [he] is well underway in dismantling American democracy, and he is doing everything he can to tear down the liberal and rules-based world order."
- Defending the 2025 prize (Aftenposten, 18 Dec 2025): "Demokrati er en forutsetning for varig fred". "Om hun står på høyresiden eller venstresiden, er ikke viktig. Det som er viktig, er om hun er for demokrati og mot diktatur." ("Whether she is on the right or the left is not important. What matters is whether she is for democracy and against dictatorship.")
- Facebook, Dec 2025 (Adresseavisen): "Is it more important to criticise Trump than to fight for democracy in Venezuela?" and "Should we demand more from them than we demand from ourselves?"
- On populism: she warned about hard, divisive rhetoric and asked whether a "Norwegian Trump" could emerge (Aftenposten column).
