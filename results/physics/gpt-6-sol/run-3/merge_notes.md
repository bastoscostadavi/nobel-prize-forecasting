# Merge notes: physics / gpt-6-sol / run-3

This consolidation uses only the 100 nomination JSON files in this run. All source files are assigned exactly once to 63 candidate groups. Counts refer to the people explicitly listed in the source `nominees` arrays, not collaborators or contextual historical names mentioned in motivations. Canonical nominees and other names are ordered by nomination frequency, with ties resolved by first appearance in lexicographically ordered source files (and array order within a file). Representative subfields use the first source's subfield.

## Ambiguous merge and split decisions

- **Atomic quantum platforms:** `bloch-immanuel.json` explicitly spans the 1995 trapped-ion quantum-computing proposal and the distinct 1998 neutral-atom optical-lattice Bose-Hubbard simulation proposal. It is retained once as the compound theoretical-foundations candidate c57 because neither narrower platform group preserves its full bundle. `wallraff-andreas.json` remains the ion-only computation candidate c45, retaining Cirac, Zoller and Blatt in source order. The three lattice-only many-body nominations (`demarco-brian.json`, `kohl-michael.json`, `lukin-mikhail.json`) form a separate candidate for Hubbard-model simulation, the superfluid-Mott transition and gas microscopy. `aspect-alain.json` retains its joint lattice/Rydberg-array discovery as a one-off because programmable optical-tweezer Rydberg arrays are a distinct experimental contribution and central to two of its three credited people. Merging all these platforms would obscure their different contributions and credits.
- **Topological insulators:** The four Kane-Mele-Molenkamp nominations share time-reversal-invariant topological insulators and the quantum spin Hall effect. `xie-xincheng.json` additionally makes zero-field quantum anomalous Hall transport in magnetic films a central discovery, credited to Qi-Kun Xue. It remains a separate joint QSH/QAH candidate: the two transport achievements have different symmetry conditions and experimental realizations in the sources. Its canonical title preserves both parts of its nomination.
- **Black-hole theory:** `thorlacius-larus.json` and `van-raamsdonk-mark.json` concern the holographic principle and AdS/CFT dictionary and are merged despite different supplementary nominees. `kim-seok.json` remains separate because its primary discovery is Strominger-Vafa microstate counting reproducing black-hole entropy; its motivation links this to subsequent holography, but merging would obscure that different calculation and its credit.
- **Exoplanets:** `queloz-didier.json` remains a joint first-transit/first-atmosphere discovery candidate, while `van-dishoeck-ewine.json` is a separate atmospheric spectroscopy candidate covering transmission theory, water detection and molecular retrieval. The latter mentions the original transit and atmospheric detection, but its main contribution and appropriate credited people extend to distinct spectroscopy methods and results.
- **Precision CMB:** `challinor-anthony.json` includes acoustic theory, WMAP and Planck; `verde-licia.json` focuses on WMAP hardware and interpretation. They are merged because both nominate precision characterization of CMB anisotropies that established the standard cosmological model, with WMAP measurements common to both. The canonical title retains acoustic structure as well as precision mapping. Inflation theory remains separate from the measurements that test it.
- **Photonic crystals:** `mork-jesper.json`, `segev-mordechai.json`, `tong-limin.json` and `vuckovic-jelena.json` share the photonic-band-gap discovery. They are merged even though the later realizations credit Noda, Joannopoulos or Russell and include nanocavities, crystal design or fibers. The common band-gap concept is the nominated core; all supplementary names are preserved. Metamaterial negative refraction/transformation optics remains separate.
- **Kilonovae:** `frebel-anna.json` combines theoretical kilonova predictions and optical detection; `kankainen-anu.json` combines merger r-process theory and kilonova modelling. They are merged because both concern the same merger-powered kilonova and heavy-element-production achievement confirmed by GW170817. Metzger and Kasen each appear twice; Foley and Lattimer each once, so Foley takes the third canonical place by first source appearance.
- **Higgs discovery:** The five ATLAS/CMS nominations are merged despite different emphasis on detector founders or discovery-era leadership. The common discovery is unchanged. Jenni appears five times, Della Negra and normalized Virdee four times each, and Gianotti twice; Gianotti is retained in `other_names`.
- **Quantum communication:** The four QKD-centered BB84/E91 nominations form c04. `hanson-ronald.json` mentions teleportation in its motivation, but explicitly nominates key distribution and entanglement-based cryptography. `zeilinger-anton.json` explicitly nominates both QKD and quantum teleportation in its discovery title and develops both contributions in its motivation; it is retained once as compound candidate c44 because c04 does not preserve that full bundle. Both groups retain Bennett, Brassard and normalized Ekert in source/frequency order. Atomic quantum memories/light-matter interfaces remain separate.
- **Neutrino astronomy:** The five IceCube nominations are merged around discovery of the high-energy astrophysical neutrino flux, despite later source-association examples and Resconi's additional credit to Albrecht Karle.
- **Related materials and sensing:** Magic-angle graphene, ultrapure hBN growth, atomically thin magnetism and quantum-Hall exciton condensation remain separate discoveries even where enabling materials or nominees overlap. Diamond NV sensing, semiconductor-dot spin qubits, dot photon sources and circuit QED likewise retain their distinct systems and achievements.
