My pick is **Michael Berry**, potentially sharing the prize with **Yakir Aharonov**, for discoveries involving quantum phases.

Berry’s work showed that a quantum system can acquire a measurable phase simply from the path it follows through a cycle of changes. Aharonov’s work revealed how electromagnetic potentials affect quantum interference. These are foundational discoveries with broad influence across modern physics—a strong fit for a Nobel recognizing work whose importance has grown over decades.

My alternative would be **Peter Shor** for quantum error correction.

Confidence is low: this is a speculative forecast, and Nobel selections are famously difficult to predict.
