# 2026 Chemistry — Codex research dossiers

72 entries, prepared 3 October 2026. Alphabetical order is not a ranking. Scientific basis is source-supported; rationale, inclusion and reservations are author judgments. Name pools are provisional, can exceed three and do not imply actual nominations. Attribution alternatives require a final contribution-level review.

<a id="density-functional-approximations"></a>

## C001 — Accurate exchange-correlation approximations for practical DFT

**Subfield:** Computational and theoretical chemistry  
**Provisional credit pool:** Axel D. Becke; John P. Perdew; Kieron Burke; Matthias Ernzerhof

**Scientific basis.** Hybrid and gradient-based functionals make electronic-structure calculations practically useful.

**Case for inclusion.** Widely used chemical prediction methods.

**Main reservation.** DFT itself won in 1998, and the pool spans different functional constructions.

**Attribution.** Becke’s hybrid lineage differs from the Perdew/Burke/Ernzerhof GGA lineage; choose a coherent scope before selecting three.

**Prior-award review:** DFT foundation: Chemistry 1998; substantial overlap review.

**Status:** Retained for substantial boundary or overlap review.

**Related entries:** [car-parrinello-dynamics](#car-parrinello-dynamics).

**Sources:** [Becke: density-functional theory of electronic structure](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jp960669l); [Perdew, Burke and Ernzerhof: generalized gradient approximation made simple](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.77.3865).
