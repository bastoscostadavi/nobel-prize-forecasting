# Nobel Committee for Physics roster (checked 2026-09-30)

## Primary source
https://www.nobelprize.org/about/the-nobel-committee-for-physics/ returns HTTP 403 to WebFetch, but loads with `curl` and a browser User-Agent. The page fetched on 2026-09-30 has the heading "Nobel Committee for Physics 2026". Its JSON-LD `dateModified` is 2026-04-07. It lists:

The page says members are elected for three-year terms from among Academy members and are assisted by specially appointed expert advisers.

## Cross-check
- kva.se's 2025 prize press release (https://www.kva.se/en/news/the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2025/) names Olle Eriksson as Chair and Göran Johansson and Eva Lindroth as members. This matches the 2026 page except for the chair: in 2026 the chair passed from Eriksson to Pearce. Eriksson is still listed as a member.
- All eight names are therefore verified for 2026 against the live nobelprize.org page. No one was dropped.

## Uncertainty
- Each member's start year and the end of their term are not given on the page. Where profiles give them, they come from other sources.
- Institutions are not on the nobelprize.org page. Each profile sources its own.
- Wikipedia's committee information is stale (for 2023 it lists Eva Olsson as chair and Gunnar Ingelman as secretary) and is not used for the 2026 roster.
