# 2026 Peace Prize: Codex independent candidate pool

Research cutoff: **3 October 2026, America/Chicago**. **108 main candidates** and **8 prior-laureate possibilities** in a separate supplement. This draft is an input to the user’s planned merge with Claude. It is unranked.

The Nobel Institute reports 287 nominations (208 individuals and 79 organizations), but keeps identities secret for 50 years. This pool cannot establish which entries were validly nominated. [Official 2026 announcement](https://www.nobelpeaceprize.org/press/press-releases/nominations-for-the-nobel-peace-prize-2026).

## Evidence labels

- **2026 expert pick**: publicly discussed by PRIO in 2026.
- **Earlier expert pick**: PRIO in 2023–2025.
- **Public 2026 nomination claim**: a nominator, nominee or reporting source says the candidate was proposed; committee acceptance is unknown.
- **2026 public speculation**: reported speculation or market discussion, with no nomination established here.
- **Author addition / independent award**: a relevant award provides evidence of a track record; the Nobel candidacy is my inference.
- **Author addition / documented work**: peace or rights work supports inclusion; the Nobel candidacy is my inference.

Current PRIO material includes the **ISS partnership**; its earlier 2026 announcement included the **WTO**. Both are retained with their correct provenance. PRIO’s list is commentary, not an official shortlist or evidence that PRIO nominated the entries. [Updated list](https://www.prio.org/nobellist/2026); [earlier announcement](https://www.prio.org/news/3693).

## Merge guidance and limitations

- This is an independent research pool for later merger, not the secret official nominee list, a probability ranking or a reconstruction of all 287 nominees.
- Valid nomination remains a necessary condition for a real 2026 award. Public statements cannot prove committee acceptance. No nomination stage was simulated.
- The ordinary deadline was 31 January 2026; committee members could add candidates at the first meeting on 26 February. An announcement after January does not prove a late submission, but it does not prove a timely one either.
- Broad author additions are included for coverage. A historical expert pick or another award is not evidence of a 2026 Nobel nomination.
- Candidate-specific reservations and the peace rationale are author judgments, not findings of the source publishers or the Nobel Committee.
- A source may be historical. No exhaustive registry audit establishes every individual as living on the cutoff date. Recheck living status, custody, roles and organization continuity for the final shortlist.
- Primary organizational pages establish mission and activity, not independent verification of effectiveness; outcomes need further review for shortlisted author additions.
- Individual and organizational versions of the same work are deliberately separate for merging, with overlap links. They must not receive duplicate nomination counts or be automatically bundled.
- No comprehensive committee-conflict audit or claim of committee disqualification is made.
- Science, technology and environmental entries with an indirect peace link are retained as distant possibilities; inclusion does not imply equal plausibility.

Use **candidate_key** and **aliases** for deduplication, then review linked person/organization cases manually. Preserve different entities such as ICJ/ICC, Save Ukraine/Save the Children, Council of Europe/EU, NUCC/NUG and ICAN civil-society network/ICAN nuclear campaign. Do not read an unknown nomination as evidence of ineligibility; do not read a public nomination as evidence of shortlist selection.
