My best prediction is **the development of next-generation DNA sequencing**, awarded to:

- **Shankar Balasubramanian**
- **David Klenerman**
- **Pascal Mayer**

Balasubramanian and Klenerman pioneered sequencing by synthesis; Mayer contributed foundational methods for massively parallel sequencing. These technologies made reading entire genomes fast and affordable, transforming biology, medicine, and biotechnology.

It’s a low-confidence forecast, but this combination of chemical invention and scientific impact makes a compelling Nobel case.
