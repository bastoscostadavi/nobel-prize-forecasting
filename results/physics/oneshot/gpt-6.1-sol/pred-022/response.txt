My prediction is **Michael Berry, possibly sharing the prize with Yakir Aharonov**, for discoveries concerning quantum phases.

Berry showed that a quantum system can acquire a measurable phase determined by the path taken through its possible configurations—the **Berry phase**. Aharonov, with David Bohm, showed that electromagnetic potentials can affect quantum interference even where the particle encounters no magnetic field. Both discoveries reshaped our understanding of quantum mechanics and underpin research in topological materials and quantum technologies.

My strongest alternative would be **Charles Bennett and Gilles Brassard**, for pioneering quantum cryptography.

I’d put low confidence in any single prediction: several discoveries deserve recognition, and the committee’s choice and timing are difficult to anticipate.
