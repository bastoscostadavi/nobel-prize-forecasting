My pick is **John Pendry**, potentially sharing the prize with **David Smith and Ulf Leonhardt**, for work on **metamaterials and transformation optics**.

Their research showed how engineered materials can manipulate electromagnetic waves in unusual ways, enabling negative refraction, proposals for “superlenses,” and experimental forms of cloaking. It combines fundamental theoretical ideas with striking experimental demonstrations—a strong Nobel profile.

My confidence is modest: Nobel selections are difficult to predict, and the committee’s deliberations are confidential. But if I had to choose one name for 2026, it would be **Pendry**.
