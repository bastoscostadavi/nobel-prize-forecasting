# Attribution and scope review of the consolidated Chemistry list

Union of the Codex draft (72 entries) and the Claude draft (106 entries), 3 October 2026, trimmed for simulation (trimmed entries are listed at the end). Shared discoveries are merged by achievement; Codex's separations (ATRP/RAFT, base/prime editing, fluorescent/phosphorescent OLEDs, Car-Parrinello/metadynamics, alkane C-H activation/selective C-H functionalization, carbon supercapacitors/MXenes) are kept. Inclusion is not a ranking.

**Input entries:** chemistry-2026-codex:C001; claude-opus-5:c38.

**Attribution judgment:** Becke’s hybrid lineage differs from the Perdew/Burke/Ernzerhof GGA lineage; choose a coherent scope before selecting three.
