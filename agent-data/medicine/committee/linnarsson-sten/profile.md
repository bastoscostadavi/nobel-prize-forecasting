## Biography

Linnarsson received his PhD from KI's Department of Medical Biochemistry and Biophysics in 2001, working on neurotrophic factors regulating neuronal survival, growth and plasticity. Instead of a postdoc he founded a company developing gene-expression analysis and single-molecule DNA sequencing methods (HDCA/SciLifeLab bio; company not named in sources found). He was appointed assistant professor at KI in 2007 and Professor of Molecular Systems Biology in 2015. He co-founded SciLifeLab's Eukaryotic Single-cell Genomics national infrastructure. At KI he has been department head (2021–2023), Dean of KI Solna (2024–2026) and Campus Vice President (2026–). Honours include the Erik K. Fernström Prize (2015), the Nicholson Lecture at Rockefeller (2017) and the Methusalem Lecture at VIB (2023). Funding from the Wallenberg Foundation, ERC, Swedish Research Council and Wellcome Trust.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- **Single-cell methods:** early single-cell RNA-seq (STRT; unique molecular identifiers, Nat Methods 2013); unbiased classification of sensory neuron types (Nat Neurosci 2014, with Ernfors).
- **Brain cell atlases:** mouse cortex and hippocampus cell types (Science 2015), oligodendrocyte heterogeneity (Science 2016), "Molecular Architecture of the Mouse Nervous System" (Cell 2018), adult human brain transcriptomic diversity and first-trimester developing brain atlases (Science 2023, BRAIN Initiative package).
- **Computational concepts:** "RNA velocity of single cells" (Nature 2018, with Peter Kharchenko).
- **Spatial methods:** co-author of spatial transcriptomics (Science 2016), developer of osmFISH and EEL FISH.
- **Current focus:** human brain development and glioblastoma heterogeneity. His stated long-term aim is the complete lineage tree of the developing human nervous system.
