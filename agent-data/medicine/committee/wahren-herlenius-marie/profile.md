## Biography

Wahren-Herlenius took a PhD in cell and molecular biology at Karolinska Institutet (1994) and her medical degree there (1995). She became docent in molecular immunology (1999) and a licensed physician (2000). She did postdoctoral and visiting work at the University of Bergen, the University of Tokyo and Harvard Medical School. She has been Professor of Experimental Rheumatology at KI since 2006 (KI profile lists 2006–2026) and was Head of the Department of Medicine, Solna, from 2021 to 2025. She was President of the Scandinavian Society for Immunology (2010–2015) and of KI's Junior Faculty (2002–2005). Honours include the Göran Gustafsson Prize in Medicine, the Nanna Svartz Prize and the IMID Prize.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- **Autoantigen biology:** Ro52/TRIM21 as an E3 ligase regulating inflammation. Loss of Ro52 induces tissue inflammation and systemic autoimmunity (J Exp Med 2009, with Vijay Kuchroo); critical review of Ro52 immunobiology (J Autoimmun 2012).
- **Congenital heart block:** showed first-degree heart block in about one-third of fetuses of anti-Ro52-positive mothers (Arthritis Rheum 2004), and further work on maternal autoantibody-mediated fetal disease and pregnancy in rheumatic disease.
- **Sjögren's disease:** ectopic germinal centres in target organs (2003); GWAS identifying immune loci (Nature Genetics 2013; 2022); genetics/epigenetics reviews (2023); co-author of the 2023 Rome consensus nomenclature ("Sjögren disease"); large international phenotype cohorts.
- **Mechanisms:** type I interferon induction by lupus autoantigen immune complexes (2006); interferon pathway and clinical outcomes (2023); "Immunopathogenic mechanisms of systemic autoimmune disease" (Lancet 2013).
- Methods: translational, combining cell biology, animal models, patient cohorts and epidemiology (her group of about 15 spans basic and clinical scientists).
