My pick is **Michael Berry and Yakir Aharonov**, for their work on **geometric and topological phases in quantum physics**.

Aharonov’s work on the **Aharonov–Bohm effect** showed that electromagnetic potentials can affect quantum interference even when particles travel through regions with no magnetic field. Berry identified the **Berry phase**, an extra phase a quantum state acquires as its environment changes around a closed cycle.

These are fundamental, experimentally established ideas with influence across condensed-matter physics, optics, and quantum technology—the kind of discoveries that can receive a Nobel decades later.
