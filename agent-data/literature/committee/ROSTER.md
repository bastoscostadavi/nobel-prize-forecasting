# Nobel Committee for Literature 2026 - Roster

Verified as of 2026-09-30 against both svenskaakademien.se and nobelprize.org ("The Nobel Committee for Literature 2026", fetched with curl). The Committee prepares the recommendation; the full Swedish Academy votes on the laureate (a majority of votes cast is needed). Committee members are elected for three years from among Academy members, and specially appointed expert advisers assist them (nobelprize.org). Since 2021 the advisers have been an anonymous regional expert group rather than external committee members (Academy press release, 2020).

## Verification of Carlberg's status and 2026 changes
- Carlberg is correctly listed. nobelprize.org 2026 lists her under "Co-opted member: Ingrid Carlberg, Permanent Secretary". The Academy's English page says "Associate member, Permanent Secretary", and its Swedish "Nobelåret" page says she is adjunct "i egenskap av ständig sekreterare". Neither source says whether she votes inside the committee. She took office as Permanent Secretary on 1 June 2026, succeeding Mats Malm (Academy press release).
- **The only 2026 change** is that Carlberg replaced Mats Malm as the co-opted member. The five full members are the same as in 2025.
- History from archived nobelprize.org pages (web.archive.org snapshots of mid-October each year):
  - 2020: Olsson (chair), Wästberg, Svenbro; co-opted Malm; external Blomqvist, Kärde, Petersen
  - 2021: Olsson, Wästberg, Svenbro, Mattson, Swärd; co-opted Malm
  - 2022–2023: Olsson, Wästberg, Sem-Sandberg, Mattson, Swärd; co-opted Malm
  - 2024: Olsson, Mattson, Sem-Sandberg, Swärd; co-opted Palm and Malm
  - 2025: Olsson, Mattson, Sem-Sandberg, Swärd, Palm; co-opted Malm
  - 2026: Olsson, Mattson, Sem-Sandberg, Swärd, Palm; co-opted Carlberg

## Uncertainty
- Whether the co-opted member (Carlberg) votes within the committee is undocumented. She votes in the full Academy.
- The start of each member's current three-year term and when it ends are not published. The "since" dates above come from annual roster snapshots.
- The full seat list for non-committee members comes from Wikipedia and has not been cross-checked seat by seat against the Academy site.
- A late change before the announcement (e.g., a resignation) cannot be ruled out, but none was found as of 2026-09-30.
