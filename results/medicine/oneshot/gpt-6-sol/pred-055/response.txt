My prediction is a prize for **chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy**: engineering a patient’s T cells to recognize and attack cancer.

I’d pick **Zelig Eshhar, Michel Sadelain, and Carl June**. Eshhar pioneered the receptor concept, Sadelain developed key designs that made the cells effective, and June helped establish their clinical impact. This is a prediction, not a report of the award.
