## Biography

Masahito Ueda was born in 1963 in Osaka and graduated from Nada High School (1982). He studied physics at the University of Tokyo (BSc 1986, MSc 1988, PhD 1991). He was a researcher at NTT Basic Research Laboratories from 1988, joined the University of Tokyo's Institute for Solid State Physics in 1992, and became associate professor at Hiroshima University in 1994. He was professor at Tokyo Institute of Technology from 2000 and moved to the University of Tokyo Department of Physics as professor in 2008 (ja-Wikipedia). His honours were not captured in the sources available here.

## Research footprint

Ueda is a broad theorist whose work connects quantum optics, cold atoms and statistical mechanics:

- **Quantum measurement and squeezing:** his NTT-era background in quantum optics and measurement theory, including work on spin squeezing (general knowledge, not re-verified).
- **Bose–Einstein condensation:** the monograph *Fundamentals and New Frontiers of Bose–Einstein Condensation* (World Scientific, 2010), and the Physics Reports review "Spinor Bose–Einstein condensates" (Kawaguchi & Ueda, 2012).
- **Information thermodynamics:** "Generalized Jarzynski Equality under Nonequilibrium Feedback Control" (Sagawa & Ueda, PRL 2010), a cornerstone of the thermodynamics of Maxwell's demon and feedback.
- **Non-Hermitian and open quantum systems:** the review "Non-Hermitian physics" (Ashida, Gong, Ueda; Advances in Physics 2020), and work on the topological classification of non-Hermitian systems.
- More recent interests include thermalization, quantum many-body dynamics and machine-learning approaches to physics (inferred from the group's trajectory).
