## Biography

Danielsson was born in 1964 and studied physics at Uppsala University. Hector Rubinstein at Stockholm University was an early mentor. Danielsson completed a PhD at Princeton University in 1992 under David Gross, working on two-dimensional string theory. After two years at CERN, he returned to Uppsala in 1994 and became a professor there in 2000. He has served as section dean for physics and as vice-rector at Uppsala. He was elected to the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences in 2009. His public activities include popular-science books, lectures and media appearances; his books include *Världen själv* (2020). Reported honours include the Göran Gustafsson Prize, the Disa Prize for popular-science writing and the King's Medal in gold.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- His early publications concern two-dimensional string theory, D-particle dynamics, holography and black-hole formation in anti-de Sitter space.
- In the early 2000s he published on possible trans-Planckian effects in inflation and their potential signatures in the cosmic microwave background.
- Later work includes de Sitter solutions in string compactifications, holographic models, and the assumptions used in constructions of dark-energy vacua.
- With Thomas Van Riet he wrote the 2018 review “What if string theory has no de Sitter vacua?”, which surveys difficulties in constructing de Sitter solutions in string theory.
- His more recent “dark bubble” papers model a four-dimensional universe as a bubble wall expanding in a five-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime. Other recent work addresses horizonless black-hole models and possible observational tests.
