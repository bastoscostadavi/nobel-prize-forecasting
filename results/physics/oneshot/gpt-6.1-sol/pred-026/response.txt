My prediction is **Hidetoshi Katori and Jun Ye**, sharing the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for their work on **optical lattice clocks**.

These clocks measure time with extraordinary precision, enabling sensitive tests of relativity and helping lay the groundwork for a future redefinition of the second. The research has the combination of fundamental physics, demonstrated impact, and decades of development that often attracts Nobel recognition.

My confidence is low: nominations are confidential, and the committee could easily favor another field. But if I had to make one prediction, that would be it.
