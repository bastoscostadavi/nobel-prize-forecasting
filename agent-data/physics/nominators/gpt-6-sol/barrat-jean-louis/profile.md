## Biography

He defended his doctoral thesis in 1987 at Université Paris 6 under Jean-Pierre Hansen, on "Structure and freezing of simple liquids and mixtures" (density-functional theory of crystallisation). His early papers on the glass transition of soft-sphere alloys (1988–1990) are co-authored with Hansen. For many years he was based at Université Lyon 1: theses.fr lists him supervising Lyon 1 doctorates from 1998 to 2011. He then moved to Grenoble, with UGA theses from 2013 onward. He received the Prix Paul Langevin of the Société Française de Physique (2000) and was elected an APS Fellow (2014). He co-authored, with Hansen, the textbook *Basic Concepts for Simple and Complex Liquids* (Cambridge University Press, 2003).

## Research footprint

- **Liquids and glasses:** density-functional theory of freezing, then molecular-dynamics studies of supercooled liquids and the glass transition.
- **Polymers and polyelectrolytes:** polyelectrolyte gels and rigid polyelectrolytes (early 1990s).
- **Interfaces and nanofluidics:** the prediction of large slip at non-wetting fluid–solid interfaces, with Bocquet (PRL 1999), which is widely cited in nanofluidics. He also studied Kapitza thermal resistance at liquid–solid interfaces and water intrusion in hydrophobic pores.
- **Mechanics of amorphous solids:** the finite-size elasticity of amorphous bodies (Tanguy et al., PRB 2002), plasticity, shear banding and yielding. He co-authored the review of mesoscale elastoplastic models (Nicolas, Ferrero, Martens and Barrat, RMP 2018).
- **Recent work (2024–2026):** toughness of multiple polymer networks, the elastic moduli of networks, intrinsically disordered proteins (with ILL), transport in nanoporous materials (Chem. Rev. 2025), topological defects in glasses (Nat. Commun. 2025), molecular motion at the glass transition (PRX 2026) and path-integral computation of thermal conductivity (PRL 2026).
