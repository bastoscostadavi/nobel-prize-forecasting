My speculative pick is **John Pendry and David R. Smith**, for pioneering **electromagnetic metamaterials and negative refraction**.

Their work showed how deliberately structured materials can manipulate electromagnetic waves in extraordinary ways, enabling effects such as negative refraction and experimental cloaking. It combines fundamental insight, experimental confirmation, and an established research field—a strong Nobel profile.

My runner-up would be **Pablo Jarillo-Herrero, Allan MacDonald, and Rafi Bistritzer**, for *magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene*: stacking graphene sheets at a particular angle produces remarkable electronic behavior, including superconductivity.

**Confidence: low.** Nobel deliberations remain secret, and the committee often rewards discoveries decades after they were made. These are my bets, not claims about a known shortlist.
