# Merge notes

This is a clerical consolidation of the 100 raw JSON nominations in `nominations/`. No existing candidate outputs, other runs, committee materials, web sources, or external knowledge were used. Every source nomination is assigned exactly once.

## Ambiguous merge and split decisions

- The three cosmic-microwave-background records were merged under precision CMB mapping because the WMAP-only records and the WMAP/Planck record describe the same overarching measurement programme and cosmological result.
- The two semiconductor-spin-qubit records were merged because both nominate the proposal and realization of electron or nuclear spin qubits in semiconductor devices; differing device emphases and scientist subsets were preserved through name counts.
- Photonic-band-gap records including photonic-crystal fibres were merged with the general photonic-crystal group because the fibre work was presented as a realization and extension of the same band-gap concept.
- `nominations/xie-xincheng.json` was kept as a separate compound candidate because it explicitly bundles the quantum spin Hall and quantum anomalous Hall effects and credits Qi-Kun Xue; forcing it into the narrower quantum-spin-Hall group would lose that contribution.
- `nominations/thorlacius-larus.json` was kept separate from the narrower AdS/CFT pair because it explicitly combines the earlier holographic principle of Gerard 't Hooft and Leonard Susskind with Maldacena's gauge/gravity realization.
- The broad Cirac–Zoller foundations record, the optical-lattice quantum-simulation records, and the quantum-network-interface record were kept separate because they center on distinct achievements despite overlapping scientists.

Ekert to Artur Ekert; Henry Snaith to Henry J. Snaith; Lyman A. Page to Lyman A. Page Jr.; Alexander Polyakov to Alexander M. Polyakov; Daniel Eisenstein to Daniel J. Eisenstein; Tejinder S. ## Source quality

All 100 source files parsed as JSON and contained the required nomination fields and one to three structured nominees. No malformed source records were found. No nomination was discarded, split, or duplicated. No scientific ranking or winner analysis was performed.
