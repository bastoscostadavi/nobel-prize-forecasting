## Biography

He trained in quantum optics at the Institut d'Optique in Philippe Grangier's group. HAL lists his 1992 doctoral thesis on quantum non-demolition (QND) measurements of optical intensity. Early papers with Grangier, Gérard Roger and Serge Reynaud (1989–1998) cover optical QND and backaction-evading measurement. He moved to the École normale supérieure de Cachan, now ENS Paris-Saclay. Since 2004 theses.fr records him as supervisor or examiner of many doctoral theses at ENS Cachan and Université Paris-Saclay. His laboratory is now LuMIn. Specific honours and academy memberships were not found in the sources consulted.

## Research footprint

- **Quantum measurement in optics:** the observation of backaction-evading intensity measurement in a three-level atomic medium (PRL 1991), a review of optical QND measurements (Appl. Phys. B 1992) and sub-shot-noise light from semiconductor emitters (PRL 1993).
- **Single-photon sources from NV centres:** direct measurement of the photon statistics of a triggered single-photon source (Treussart et al., PRL 2002).
- **Foundations:** the experimental realisation of Wheeler's delayed-choice gedanken experiment with single photons (Jacques et al., Science 2007, with Grangier and Aspect).
- **NV quantum sensing:** the review "Magnetometry with nitrogen-vacancy defects in diamond" (Rondin et al., Rep. Prog. Phys. 2014). With CEA he did NV magnetometry inside diamond anvil cells, measuring micrometre-sized samples under high pressure (Lesik et al., Science 2019) and working up to 130 GPa (PRB 2023).
- **Recent work:** NV-based radio-frequency spectrum analysers (Commun. Eng. 2022), all-carbon electrical readout (2023, 2025), levitated nanodiamonds, and SiV centres in nanodiamonds.
