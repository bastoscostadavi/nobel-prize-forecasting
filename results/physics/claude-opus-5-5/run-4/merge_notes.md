# Merge notes

## Ambiguous merge and split decisions

- `bloch-immanuel.json` bundles the theoretical foundations of trapped-ion quantum computation with neutral-atom and optical-lattice quantum simulation. It was retained as a compound one-off candidate because assigning it to either narrower group would discard the other part of the nomination.
- `fang-zhong.json` and `samuelson-lars.json` genuinely bundle the quantum spin Hall and quantum anomalous Hall effects. They were retained together as a compound candidate, separate from both the narrower quantum-spin-Hall group and the focused `wang-enge.json` quantum-anomalous-Hall nomination.
- `bergholtz-emil.json` and `haldane-duncan.json` were merged as the fractional quantum anomalous Hall effect despite using different moiré or graphene platforms, because both nominate the same zero-field fractional Chern-insulator discovery.
- `flensberg-karsten.json` was kept separate from `simmons-michelle.json`: the former focuses on experimental realization and control of quantum-dot electron-spin qubits, while the latter bundles the foundational quantum-dot and donor-in-silicon proposals.
- `lu-chaoyang.json` was kept separate from the foundational quantum-key-distribution nominations because it focuses on long-distance fibre and satellite entanglement distribution and global quantum networks with a distinct nominee set.
