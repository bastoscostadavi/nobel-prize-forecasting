My speculative pick is **Michael Berry and Yakir Aharonov**, sharing the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for their work on **quantum phases**.

The Aharonov–Bohm effect and Berry phase revealed subtle ways that electromagnetic potentials and a system’s evolution affect quantum interference. Both discoveries are experimentally established and have broad influence across condensed matter physics, optics, and quantum technology—strong ingredients for a Nobel.

My runner-up would be **Charles Kane, Eugene Mele, and Laurens Molenkamp**, for predicting and experimentally establishing topological insulators.

Confidence is low: Nobel nominations remain confidential for 50 years, so this is a judgment about scientific merit and timing, rather than a known shortlist.
