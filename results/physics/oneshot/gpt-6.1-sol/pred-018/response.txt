My pick is **John Pendry and David R. Smith**, for their work on **metamaterials and transformation optics**, potentially sharing the prize with a third researcher.

Their work showed how engineered structures can control electromagnetic waves in ways ordinary materials cannot—including negative refraction and limited forms of electromagnetic cloaking. It has the combination Nobel committees often recognize: a fundamental conceptual advance, experimental demonstrations, and decades of scientific influence.

My confidence is low: Nobel nominations remain confidential for 50 years, and I can’t verify the latest 2026 developments here. A strong alternative would be **Yakir Aharonov and Michael Berry**, for discoveries concerning quantum phases and their physical consequences.
