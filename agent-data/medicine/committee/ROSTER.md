## Verification (2026-09-30)
- The nobelprize.org committee page returns 403 to WebFetch but loads with `curl` and a browser user agent. It lists exactly these six people with the titles above (Perlmann as "Secretary-General").
- The Nobel Assembly site (https://www.nobelprizemedicine.org/the-nobel-committee/, updated 2026-03-16) states: "The Nobel Committee consists of five members and the Secretary General of the Nobel Assembly." Its "Nobel Committee 2026" photo caption seats Wahren-Herlenius, Linnarsson, Svenningsson (chairman), El Manira and Sandberg.
- **Wahren-Herlenius is confirmed** by both primary sources. She is presumably new in 2026, replacing Olle Kämpe (2025 chair), who no longer appears. This is inferred.
- **No missing member.** The "7 members" in the Karolinska hospital article summary is not supported by the primary pages. The committee has 5 elected members plus the Secretary-General, which is 6. The earlier "7" was probably a summarisation error, so no new profile was created.

## Associate (adjunct) members 2026
Each year about ten associate members are elected for March–October. They take part in the committee's work but are not full members. The 2026 photo caption also shows the following people. It does not label each person's role, so treat these as the likely associate members for 2026:
Pär Nordlund, Randall Johnson, Qiang Pan-Hammarström, Christer Betsholtz, Anna Wedell, Juleen Zierath, Birgitta Henriques Normark, András Simon, Janne Johansson, Gonçalo Castelo-Branco, Gunilla Karlsson Hedestam, Patrik Ernfors (not pictured). That is 12 names against "ten associate members", so the list may include others, such as Assembly officers. They are not profiled here.

## Assembly
The Nobel Assembly at KI has 50 members who vote on the prize (first Monday in October) on the Committee's proposal. Individual Assembly members are not profiled here. The Committee also uses ad hoc external experts each year; they are not identified.
