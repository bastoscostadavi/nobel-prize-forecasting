My prediction is **the invention of next-generation DNA sequencing**, awarded to:

- **Shankar Balasubramanian**
- **David Klenerman**
- **Pascal Mayer**

A plausible citation: *“For the development of massively parallel methods for DNA sequencing.”*

Balasubramanian and Klenerman pioneered sequencing by synthesis; Mayer helped develop DNA-colony technology that enabled massively parallel sequencing. Together, these advances made reading genomes dramatically faster and cheaper, transforming biology and medicine.

This is a low-confidence prediction, but the technology’s mature, enormous impact makes it a strong Nobel candidate.
