My prediction: **Steven Berry, James Levinsohn, and Ariel Pakes**, for **developing empirical methods to study demand and competition in markets with differentiated products**.

Their landmark contribution is the **Berry–Levinsohn–Pakes (BLP) framework**, which lets economists estimate how consumers substitute among competing products, then analyze pricing, market power, mergers, and consumer welfare. It became a foundational tool in empirical industrial organization, particularly for markets such as automobiles.

My rationale: this is a mature, influential contribution with a clear three-person attribution and substantial applications to competition policy—characteristics that make it a strong Nobel candidate.
