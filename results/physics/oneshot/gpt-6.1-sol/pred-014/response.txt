My pick is **Michael Berry and Yakir Aharonov**, for revealing how geometry and electromagnetic potentials shape quantum behavior.

Aharonov’s work showed that electromagnetic potentials can affect particles even where the electromagnetic field is zero. Berry identified a geometric phase that quantum states acquire as their surroundings change. Both discoveries became foundational, with consequences across condensed matter, optics, and quantum computing—a strong fit for Nobel recognition.

My runner-up would be **John Pendry and David Smith**, for metamaterials and negative refraction.

Confidence is low: these are plausible scientific candidates, and Nobel nominations remain confidential for 50 years.
