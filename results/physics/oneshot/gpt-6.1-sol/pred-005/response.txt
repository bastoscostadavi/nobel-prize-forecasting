**My pick is Michael Berry, possibly sharing the prize with Yakir Aharonov**, for discoveries concerning quantum phases—how changes in a quantum system’s phase can produce observable physical effects.

Berry’s geometric phase and the Aharonov–Bohm effect are foundational, experimentally established discoveries with broad influence across modern physics. They have the combination of depth, lasting impact, and maturity that Nobel committees often recognize.
