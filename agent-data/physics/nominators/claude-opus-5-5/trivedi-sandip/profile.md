## Biography

Trivedi was born in India on 5 April 1963. He received an integrated M.Sc. in physics from IIT Kanpur (1985) and a PhD from Caltech (1990) under John Preskill. He was a postdoc at the Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton (1990–1992). His later career was at TIFR, where he served as Director (Wikipedia gives no dates) and is now Distinguished Professor. His awards include:
- Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize (2005)
- Infosys Prize in Physical Sciences (2010)
- TWAS Prize in Physics (2015)
- H. K. Firodia Award (2018)

He serves on the program advisory board of the International Centre for Theoretical Sciences (ICTS). His spouse is neuroscientist Shubha Tole (Wikipedia).

## Research footprint

Trivedi is best known as the "T" in KKLT: Kachru, Kallosh, Linde and Trivedi, "De Sitter vacua in string theory" (*Phys. Rev. D*, 2003; ~3,700 INSPIRE citations). The paper constructed metastable de Sitter vacua with stabilised moduli in type IIB flux compactifications. It was followed by the KKLMMT paper on brane inflation (2003, ~1,400 citations) and by earlier work on moduli stabilisation from fluxes in IIB orientifolds (2002). His other work includes:
- Black holes: limits of the statistical description (with Preskill, Wilczek and others, 1991), non-supersymmetric attractors (2005), and holography of charged dilaton black holes (2009).
- AdS/CFT: holographic probes of AdS (1998) and Chern–Simons theories with vector fermion matter (with Giombi, Minwalla, Prakash and Wadia, 2011).

Recent INSPIRE entries (2023–2026) cover JT gravity and the problem of time in de Sitter space, dS/CFT holography, the thermodynamics of cosmological horizons, SYK models and quantum chaos, and entanglement in internal spaces. The arc runs from string phenomenology and compactification, through black hole physics and holography, to quantum aspects of de Sitter space.
