My prediction: **Steven Berry, James Levinsohn, and Ariel Pakes**, for **developing methods to measure demand and competition in markets with differentiated products**.

Their “BLP” framework lets economists use prices, sales, and product characteristics to estimate how consumers choose among competing products. It helps answer consequential questions: How would a merger affect prices? How much market power do firms have? What do consumers gain from new products?

I favor this contribution because it combines a foundational methodological advance with widespread practical use in competition policy and empirical economics.

This is a low-confidence forecast, but it is my strongest single pick.
