# Sample note

This is a judgment-based sample of 100 living scientists who plausibly could have submitted a nomination for the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics. It is weighted toward professors at universities outside the Nordic countries (63), reflecting the Nobel Committee's practice of inviting thousands of scholars worldwide. It also includes Nordic professors (19), members of the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (9), Physics laureates (7), and other scientists who could receive a discretionary invitation (2). The sample spans 82 institutions, 24 countries, and a range of physics subfields; it emphasizes breadth of the eligible nominator pool rather than public prominence.

The actual 2026 nominator list and its proportions are confidential for 50 years. Membership in an eligible group, or a plausible fit for an invitation, does not establish that a person received an invitation or submitted a nomination. All members and co-opted members on the published 2026 Nobel Committee for Physics roster were excluded.

Sources: [Nobel Prize nomination and selection rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/physics/) and [2026 Nobel Committee for Physics roster](https://www.nobelprize.org/about/the-nobel-committee-for-physics).
