## Research footprint

Challinor works on testing the cosmological model with the cosmic microwave background. His KICC page describes a focus on "the origin, interpretation, and measurement of temperature anisotropies and polarization in the CMB". Two contributions stand out:

- **CAMB:** "Efficient computation of CMB anisotropies in closed FRW models" (Lewis, Challinor & Lasenby, ApJ 2000). This paper introduced the Boltzmann code that became a standard tool in the field (about 4,400 Crossref citations).
- **CMB lensing:** "Weak gravitational lensing of the CMB" (Lewis & Challinor, Physics Reports 2006) is the standard review, and he has continued to develop lensing estimators (2025).

He was a Core Team member of the Planck High-Frequency Instrument. He co-authored the Planck 2013, 2015 and 2018 cosmological-parameter and inflation papers, the joint BICEP2/Keck–Planck analysis (2015) and "The Simons Observatory: science goals and forecasts" (2019). He is now a Simons Observatory member working on lensing, cosmological parameters and the search for primordial B-mode polarization from gravitational waves. Recent work also covers kSZ velocity reconstruction and galaxy bispectra (2026).
