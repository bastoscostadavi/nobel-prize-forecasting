My prediction is **Steven Berry, James Levinsohn, and Ariel Pakes**, for **developing methods to measure consumer demand and competition in markets with differentiated products**.

Their influential “BLP” framework lets economists use market data—such as car prices, features, and sales—to estimate consumer preferences, firms’ market power, and the likely effects of mergers. It helped make empirical industrial organization a practical tool for competition policy.

This is a low-confidence forecast: Nobel selections are difficult to predict, but this contribution has the intellectual importance, demonstrated usefulness, and longevity of a plausible winner.
