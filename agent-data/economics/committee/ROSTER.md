## Resolved questions
- **Strömberg:** nobelprize.org lists him as a current Member for 2026. The SSE page's "former member" wording reflects his earlier spell, which ended around 2022, and is out of date. Why he returned is not documented.
- **Friberg:** he is Co-opted, not a Member. The SSE page's "member" wording is loose.
- **Fredriksson:** Uppsala is confirmed by his KVA member page and personal site.
- **Enflo:** Lund, Economic History, is confirmed by the Lund pages and "Professor in economic history" on nobelprize.org.
- **Hjalmarsson:** Gothenburg is confirmed by the GU staff page and her personal site.
- **Teorell:** Stockholm University is confirmed by his SU profile, the SU Nobel-committees page and KVA. He was at Lund 2006–2021 and has been at SU since 2021.
- **Co-opted vs member:** several co-opted people call themselves "members" in their CVs and news items (Boppart, Enflo, Hjalmarsson). The profiles use the nobelprize.org classification.

## Public roles in past prize cycles (nobelprize.org ceremony-speech and prize-announcement pages)
- **Presentation speeches:** 2016 and 2017 Strömberg; 2018 Hassler; 2020 Andersson; 2021 Fredriksson; 2023 Enflo; 2024 Teorell; 2025 Hassler.
- **Post-announcement interviews:** 2012 Krusell; 2013 Strömberg; 2018 Krusell; 2020 Andersson; 2022 Hassler; 2023 Hjalmarsson; 2024 Teorell; 2025 Enflo.
- **Scientific Backgrounds** are signed collectively by "The Committee". PDF metadata or stamps show weak traces of individual drafting: 2017 has "Per Strömberg" in the author field, and 2025 carries a "Timo Boppart 2025/09/08" stamp. These are inferences only.

## Uncertainty
- Term end dates are unknown for everyone. Co-opted members may rotate year to year.
- Start years come mostly from personal CVs or secondary sources, not from official KVA documents.
- Dolton & Tol give Teorell as starting in 2025, which conflicts with his 2024 speech and interview. Their table is treated as low-reliability.
- Krusell: a Dec 2025 SU news item says he "has chaired" the committee since 2021. This conflicts with nobelprize.org and his CV, which both say secretary.
