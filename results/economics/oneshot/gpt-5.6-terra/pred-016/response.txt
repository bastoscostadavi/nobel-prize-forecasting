My prediction: **Joel Mokyr, Philippe Aghion, and Peter Howitt**.

Likely citation theme: **explaining long-run economic growth through innovation, creative destruction, and the cultural/institutional conditions that sustain technological progress.**

Mokyr supplies the economic-history account of why modern innovation became self-reinforcing; Aghion and Howitt formalized and developed the Schumpeterian growth framework. This would make for an unusually coherent three-person prize.
