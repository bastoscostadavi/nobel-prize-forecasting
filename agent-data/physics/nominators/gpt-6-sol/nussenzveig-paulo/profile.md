## Biography

Paulo Alberto Nussenzveig is a professor at the Instituto de Física of the Universidade de São Paulo. He is the son of the theoretical physicist H. Moysés Nussenzveig (1932/33–2022), about whom he wrote the tribute "Meu pai" in Jornal da USP. He wrote a fortnightly Rádio USP column, "Ciência e Cientistas," for about four years, ending it in February 2022 when he was appointed USP's Pró-Reitor de Pesquisa (later Pesquisa e Inovação). In that role he led the pró-reitoria's strategic-objectives process, which included revising USP's research good-practice guide. ORCID records that a doctoral thesis he advised, on multicolour entanglement between intense light beams, received the CAPES Thesis Award (2008 edition). His own degrees are not documented in the sources retrieved.

## Research footprint

Nussenzveig co-leads a quantum optics laboratory at IF-USP with Marcelo Martinelli. His early USP work (2000–2003) covered cavity-QED effects on cold-atom collisions, a classical analogue of electromagnetically induced transparency (*Am. J. Phys.* 70, 2002), and noise correlations in EIT. The group's signature programme is continuous-variable entanglement of bright beams from above-threshold optical parametric oscillators. Milestones include:

- two-colour entanglement (2005);
- tripartite pump–signal–idler entanglement (*PRL* 97, 140504, 2006, with Claude Fabre);
- "Three-color entanglement" (*Science* 326, 823, 2009);
- hexapartite entanglement (*PRL* 121, 073601, 2018).

More recent work covers four-wave-mixing OPOs in hot atomic vapour (*PRL* 125, 083601, 2020, with A. M. Marino and H. M. Florez), diagnostics for continuous-variable cluster states, and visible and near-visible microresonator nonlinear photonics (*Optica*, 2020–2021).

## Public statements and values

- On taking office (Feb 2022), he called the Royal Society motto *Nullius in verba* "a great description of the scientific enterprise: find out for yourself, question authority." He quoted Einstein on fate making him an authority as punishment for his contempt of authority (Rádio USP; translated from Portuguese).
- He said he felt "excited and scared" about the pró-reitoria, excited because of "the opportunities to contribute actively to improving research conditions at USP" (same source, translated).
