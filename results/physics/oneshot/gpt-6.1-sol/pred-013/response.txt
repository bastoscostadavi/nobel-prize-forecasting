My pick is **Charles L. Kane, Eugene J. Mele, and Laurens W. Molenkamp**, for their work on **topological insulators**—materials that insulate in their interiors while conducting electricity along their edges or surfaces.

Kane and Mele supplied foundational theoretical predictions, and Molenkamp’s team provided a landmark experimental demonstration. That combination of a surprising prediction, convincing experiments, and lasting influence makes a strong Nobel case.

My confidence is modest. The 2016 prize already recognized other work on topology in matter, which could weigh against this choice, and Nobel timing is notoriously difficult to predict.
