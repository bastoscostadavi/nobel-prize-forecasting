# Merge notes

This is a clerical consolidation of the 100 raw JSON nominations in `nominations/`. No existing candidate outputs, other runs, committee materials, web sources, or external knowledge were used. Every source nomination is assigned exactly once.

## Ambiguous merge and split decisions

- The Planck CMB nomination was kept separate from the four WMAP nominations because it names a different mission, measurement leadership, and recipient slate; the four WMAP records were merged.
- The two broad controlled-atom records were merged as quantum information processing and simulation with trapped ions and cold atoms. The three narrower optical-lattice Hubbard-simulation records remain a separate group because they center on the superfluid–Mott-insulator transition and quantum-gas microscopy.
- `nominations/nussenzveig-paulo.json` was kept as a separate compound candidate because it combines squeezed light with continuous-variable teleportation and optical cluster states; the three nominations centered on squeezed-light-enhanced precision interferometry were merged separately.
- The laser-only and laser/beam-driven plasma-wakefield records were merged because both center on plasma wakefield acceleration; the broader source's additional beam-driven emphasis is retained in provenance and nominee counts.
- `nominations/xie-xincheng.json` was kept as a separate compound candidate because it explicitly bundles quantum spin Hall and quantum anomalous Hall effects and credits Qi-Kun Xue.
