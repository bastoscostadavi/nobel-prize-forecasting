## Biography

Born 14 October 1971 in Venice, Italy. She earned a Laurea from the University of Padua (1996) and a PhD from the University of Edinburgh (2000), working with Sabino Matarrese and Alan Heavens. She was a research associate at Princeton (2000–2003) and a faculty member at the University of Pennsylvania (2003–2007). She has been ICREA Professor at the University of Barcelona since 2007/2008. She also held a Professor II post at the University of Oslo (2013–2016) and was a Radcliffe Fellow at Harvard (2015–2016). Since 2019 she has chaired the arXiv Science Advisory Board, and since 2020 she has been scientific director of *JCAP*. Her honours include:
- Gruber Cosmology Prize (2012) and Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics (2018), both shared with the WMAP team
- Three ERC grants (Starting 2009, Consolidator 2016, Synergy "RedH0t" 2025)
- National Research Award of Catalonia (2018) and Rey Jaime I Award (2021)
- Medal of the Spanish Royal Physics Society (2024)

(Wikipedia.)

## Research footprint

Verde was a key member of the WMAP science team. She co-authored the first-year WMAP cosmological-parameters paper (Spergel, Verde et al., *ApJS* 148, 2003; ~9,300 Crossref citations), which helped establish the standard ΛCDM model. Her early work used higher-order statistics (the bispectrum) of the 2dF galaxy redshift survey to show that galaxies trace the dark-matter distribution. She later worked on:
- baryon acoustic oscillations and SDSS-III/BOSS analyses
- model-independent reconstructions of the expansion history and dark-energy properties
- neutrino mass constraints from cosmology
- systematic-error control in precision cosmology

Her 2019 *Nature Astronomy* perspective with Treu and Riess, "Tensions between the early and late Universe" (~1,200 citations), is a central reference on the Hubble tension. Her current ERC Synergy project targets that tension directly. Her 2026 preprints cover lensed high-redshift star clusters as cosmic clocks, bispectrum BAO, and Bayesian evidence for the neutrino mass ordering. She is involved in DESI and Euclid (home page). Over time her focus has moved from CMB and survey statistics toward stress-testing ΛCDM through independent probes.
