My prediction is **Jun Ye and Hidetoshi Katori**, for their pioneering work on **optical lattice clocks**.

These clocks use atoms trapped by laser light to measure time with extraordinary precision. They enable sensitive tests of relativity and could underpin a future redefinition of the second. That combination of fundamental physics, technical achievement, and lasting impact makes their work a strong Nobel candidate.

My confidence is low—Nobel selections are secret, and deserving discoveries can wait decades. But if I had to make one bet for the 2026 Physics prize, it would be **Ye and Katori sharing it**.
