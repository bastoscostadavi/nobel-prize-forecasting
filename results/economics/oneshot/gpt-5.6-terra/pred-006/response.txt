My prediction: **Philippe Aghion and Peter Howitt**.

Contribution: the development of **Schumpeterian endogenous-growth theory**—showing how innovation, creative destruction, competition, and policy shape long-run economic growth. Their work made technological progress an explicit, analysable outcome of economic incentives rather than an unexplained external force.

It would be a natural complement to Paul Romer’s 2018 prize: Romer formalized ideas-driven growth; Aghion and Howitt explained the continual replacement of old technologies by new ones.
