## Verification
- The official nobelprize.org page returns 403 to WebFetch, but a plain `curl` with a browser User-Agent returned it (HTTP 200) on 2026-09-30. It lists exactly the 8 people above under "Nobel Committee for Chemistry 2026", with the roles shown.
- Every member's institution and committee membership is also confirmed on their KVA contact page (kva.se/en/contact/<name>/).
- Archived nobelprize.org rosters (Wayback Machine, Sept snapshots of 2022-2025) were used for committee history.

## Resolved open questions
- **Andrei Chabes:** CONFIRMED as a 2026 member (official page, KVA contact page). Earlier doubt came from a failed search, not from contrary evidence.
- **Olof Ramström:** NOT a 2026 member. He was an ordinary member in 2022 and 2023 and a co-opted member in 2024 and 2025. He was the expert member who presented the 2025 (and 2022) prize at kva.se announcements, which explains the kva.se 2025 mention. He is absent from the 2026 list, and his KVA contact page no longer lists committee membership. No directory created.
- **Wikipedia roster** (Åqvist as chair; Claes Gustafsson, Ramström, Pernilla Wittung-Stafshede) matches the 2022 roster, so it is stale. Gustafsson left after 2022, and Wittung-Stafshede and Ramström after 2025.
- **Institutions:** all verified. Correction: Peter Somfai is at **Lund University**, not Stockholm (he moved there from KTH in 2012).

## Remaining uncertainty
- Term end dates are not published. Committee members serve three-year terms, and the exact start years for Linke and Somfai (before 2022) are not known.
- The reason for co-opted vs ordinary status changes (Brzezinski, Åqvist) is not documented. It is probably the end of elected terms (inference).
- The 2021 roster was not checked. Åqvist's 2021 chairmanship rests on Wikipedia.
