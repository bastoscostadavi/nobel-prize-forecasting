## Research footprint

The IMR page summarises his group's programme as "Emergent Properties of Correlated π-electrons in Flexible Assembly of Organic Nanostructures." It studies electronic states from superconductivity to insulating ground states in organic molecular materials, where soft lattices and strong electron correlations compete.

Signature contributions:
- Real-space infrared imaging of metal–insulator phase separation near the bandwidth-controlled Mott transition in κ-(BEDT-TTF)2Cu[N(CN)2]Br (*JPSJ* 74, 2005), using synchrotron infrared microscopy.
- Systematic use of X-ray irradiation to introduce controlled molecular disorder into organic Mott insulators and superconductors. He reviewed this work in "Mott-Anderson Transition in Molecular Conductors" (*Crystals* 2012).
- Dielectric and noise spectroscopy of dimer-Mott insulators, charge glass and charge order.

His KAKEN keywords span:
- Mott transitions and quantum spin liquids
- charge glass and charge order
- infrared and synchrotron spectroscopy
- resistance-noise spectroscopy
- vortex-lattice melting in organic superconductors
- Dirac electrons
- proton dynamics

Recent work includes elastic strain tuning of geometric frustration in an organic quantum magnet (*Science Advances* 2025, with Winter, Saito and others) and magneto-optics of an organic altermagnet candidate (2024).
