## Biography

Born in Rabat, Morocco, in 1965, El Manira holds Moroccan and Swedish citizenship. He earned a BSc in biology at Mohammed V University (1988) and a PhD in neuroscience at Aix-Marseille University (1992), then joined KI's Department of Neuroscience as a postdoc. He started his independent group at KI in 1994, received the Swedish Research Council's Elite Research Award (2003), became full professor in 2005, and received a Distinguished Professor Grant (2017) and an ERC Advanced Grant. He is a member of the Hassan II Academy of Sciences and Technologies (2013), the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (2015) and Academia Europaea (2017), and an EMBO member. He has chaired the Swedish Research Council's Scientific Council for Medicine and holds Moroccan state honours (all per Wikipedia).

## Research / intellectual footprint

- **Lamprey era (1990s–2000s):** vertebrate central pattern generators, work linked to Sten Grillner's lamprey model, e.g. "Neural networks that co-ordinate locomotion and body orientation in lamprey" (TINS 1995), and neuromodulation (nitric oxide, endocannabinoid) of spinal locomotor networks.
- **Zebrafish era (2010s–):** modular organisation of spinal circuits controlling speed. Distinct V2a interneuron/motoneuron modules (Neuron 2014); motor neurons retrogradely controlling the rhythm generator via gap junctions (Nature 2016); three rhythm-generating modules acting like "gears" (Neuron 2020); molecular blueprints for speed modules (Nat Neurosci 2023); brainstem circuits for start, speed and steering (2022, 2025).
- **Synthesis:** co-authored "Current Principles of Motor Control" with Grillner (Physiol Rev 2019) and "Circuit Modules for Flexible Locomotion" (Annu Rev Neurosci 2025).
- **Collaborative work:** pain and Schwann-cell sensory biology with Ernfors (Science 2019; Nat Neurosci 2026), and an intraspinal Piezo2+ proprioceptive organ.
- Methods: whole-cell electrophysiology, circuit mapping, genetics and transcriptomics in small vertebrates.

## Public statements and values
