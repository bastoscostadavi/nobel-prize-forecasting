## Biography

Olsson studied engineering physics at Chalmers and completed doctoral research there on interfacial structures in zinc-oxide varistor materials. She subsequently worked with David R. Clarke at IBM in the United States and returned to Chalmers, becoming docent in 1991 and associate professor in 1996. She was professor at Uppsala University from 1997 before returning to Chalmers as full professor. At Chalmers she has held leadership roles in materials analysis, microscopy, applied physics and the Division of Nano and Biophysics, and has chaired the Faculty Senate. She was elected to the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences in 2011. International appointments include a JSPS professorial fellowship and a University of Tokyo fellowship. Kyushu University identified her as president of the International Federation of Societies for Microscopy in 2024.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- Her research develops and applies transmission electron microscopy, including in situ experiments, specimen manipulation, quantitative imaging and spectroscopy.
- Materials studied in her publication record include zinc-oxide grain boundaries, nanoparticles, oxide interfaces, catalysts, photovoltaic and thermoelectric materials, organic thin films and components relevant to quantum devices.
- A well-cited part of this work concerns oxygen vacancies and interface structure in lanthanum-aluminate/strontium-titanate systems.
- Her group has also reported electron-beam-induced structural changes at gold surfaces and has worked on plasmon–exciton coupling.
- Her projects span physics, materials science, chemistry and engineering and involve both instrument methods and material-specific questions.
