## Biography

Sandberg trained entirely at Karolinska Institutet before his postdoc: Master of Medical Science (2000), licentiate (2002) and PhD (2004) in the Department of Microbiology, Tumor and Cell Biology. He was a postdoctoral fellow at MIT (2005–2007), where he co-authored work with Christopher Burge's group on alternative isoform regulation. He returned to KI as assistant professor (2008–2013), associate professor (2013–2015) and professor of molecular genetics (2015–). Honours: EMBO Young Investigator (2012), Sven and Ebba-Christina Hagberg Prize (2012), Anders Jahre's Prize for Young Researchers (2014), EMBO membership (2019), Nobel Assembly membership (2019), and a Torsten Söderberg Academy Professorship in Medicine (2022). He was a STIAS fellow in 2024.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- **RNA processing (postdoc):** "Alternative isoform regulation in human tissue transcriptomes" (Nature 2008); proliferating cells express mRNAs with shortened 3′UTRs (Science 2008); CTCF-promoted Pol II pausing links DNA methylation to splicing (Nature 2011).
- **Single-cell methods:** Smart-seq (Nat Biotechnol 2012), Smart-seq2 (Nat Methods 2013; Nat Protoc 2014), Tn5 tagmentation (Genome Res 2014), Smart-seq3 and Smart-seq3xpress (Nat Biotechnol 2022).
- **Biology from single cells:** random monoallelic expression (Science 2014), human preimplantation embryo lineage and X-chromosome dynamics (Cell 2016), human pancreatic islets in type 2 diabetes (Cell Metab 2016), transcriptional bursting kinetics (Nature Genetics 2022 on lncRNAs; Nat Cell Biol 2024 on new-RNA sequencing).
- **Recent collaborations:** adult human hippocampal neurogenesis (Science 2025, with Frisén), aging liver, haematopoiesis.
