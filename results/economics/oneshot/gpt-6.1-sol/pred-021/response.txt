My prediction is **Elhanan Helpman, Gene Grossman, and Marc Melitz**, for explaining how firms, global production, and trade policy shape international trade.

A plausible prize citation: *“For advancing our understanding of international trade through theories of firm heterogeneity and global production.”*

- **Helpman:** How multinational firms, outsourcing, and firms’ organizational choices shape trade.
- **Grossman:** How offshoring and political interests influence trade patterns and trade policy.
- **Melitz:** Why more productive firms export, and how opening to trade reallocates activity across firms, raising aggregate productivity.

This combination connects foundational trade theory to globalization’s effects on firms and workers. It’s a speculative forecast; my confidence in the exact trio is low.
