My prediction: **Elhanan Helpman, Gene Grossman, and Marc Melitz**, for **explaining how firms and political incentives shape international trade**.

- **Helpman:** Explaining trade through economies of scale, product differentiation, and multinational firms.
- **Grossman:** Explaining how lobbying and political interests determine trade protection, notably through his work with Helpman.
- **Melitz:** Showing how trade reallocates activity toward more productive firms: only some firms export, and opening markets changes an industry’s overall productivity.

The unifying contribution would be explaining **which firms benefit from globalization, how trade changes productivity, and why governments choose particular trade policies**.

This is a low-confidence forecast. Nobel selections are difficult to predict, but this trio offers a coherent, influential body of work with clear economic consequences.
