My pick is **John Pendry**, potentially sharing the prize with **David R. Smith and Nader Engheta**, for pioneering **metamaterials**—engineered materials that manipulate electromagnetic waves in extraordinary ways.

Pendry’s work on negative refraction and the “perfect lens,” alongside advances in transformation optics, offers a compelling Nobel narrative: fundamental theory that opened a major experimental field.

My strongest alternative is **Ignacio Cirac and Peter Zoller**, for foundational proposals for quantum computing and quantum simulation using trapped atoms and ions.

Confidence is low: Nobel deliberations are secret, and several discoveries have equally strong claims.
