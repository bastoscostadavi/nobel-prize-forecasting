## Research footprint

Tanaka is a theorist of superconducting junctions and of how pairing symmetry appears at surfaces and interfaces. His *PRL* with Satoshi Kashiwaya, "Theory of tunneling spectroscopy of d-wave superconductors" (1995; ~1,100 Crossref citations), explained the zero-bias conductance peak from midgap Andreev bound states. It became a standard tool for identifying unconventional pairing in the cuprates. He extended this framework to:
- anisotropic superconductors (*PRB* 1996)
- spin-triplet superconductor junctions and the anomalous proximity effect (*PRB* 2004)
- Sr2RuO4

With Masatoshi Sato and Naoto Nagaosa he wrote the review "Symmetry and Topology in Superconductors – Odd-Frequency Pairing and Edge States" (*JPSJ* 2012; ~560 citations), linking topological edge states to odd-frequency Cooper pairs. His KAKEN keywords track this evolution from d-wave tunnelling and Josephson effects to Majorana fermions, topological superconductivity, Weyl/Dirac semimetals and odd-frequency pairs. His 2025–2026 work covers:
- altermagnet/superconductor hybrids and spin-polarized Josephson currents
- Bogoliubov flat bands in twisted materials
- paired fractional quantum Hall transport
- disorder-robust trivial Majorana-like states
