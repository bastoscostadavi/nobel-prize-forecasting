## Verification findings (2026-09-30)
- **Toje's nominating party resolved: FrP.** The FrP parliamentary group nominated him in Feb 2018 after the Storting rejected Carl I. Hagen (NRK: https://www.nrk.no/urix/frp-vil-ha-forskningsdirektor-asle-toje-i-nobelkomiteen-1.13904428 ; VG). The no.wikipedia member list also shows "Fremskrittspartiet".
- **Frydnes: Labour seat.** SNL says "nominert av Arbeiderpartiet". He replaced Thorbjørn Jagland (NRK 2020). The earlier Høyre attribution was wrong.
- **Enger: Centre Party** (NRK 21 Nov 2017; Nationen 19 Oct 2020). The earlier "Venstre" attribution was wrong.
- **Clemet: Høyre** (Aftenposten 18 Nov 2020). **Larsen: Labour**, nominated 29 Nov 2023 to succeed Berit Reiss-Andersen (Adresseavisen/NTB).
- **No membership change found in 2026.** The official interests register dated 25 Mar 2026 lists the same five members plus Harpviken as secretary. The announcement page for the 2026 prize (Fri 9 Oct 2026, 11:00 CEST) names Frydnes as chair. The Storting election of members for 2027–2032, replacing or re-electing Frydnes, Enger and Clemet, had not been found. It is normally held in late autumn (Dec 2020 for the 2021–26 term), after the prize is announced.
- **2026 context:**
  - The committee adopted ethics rules on 22 Mar 2026 and published its first interests register, following the Jagland/Epstein revelations (E24).
  - In Feb–Mar 2026 Toje faced calls to resign (MDG leader Arild Hermstad) over his misstated 2003 contact with David Irving. He admitted this ("Ja, det har jeg gjort", Aftenposten, 3 Mar 2026) but remained vice chair.
- Birth year conflict for Toje: nobelpeaceprize.org gives 1975, Wikipedia gives 16 Feb 1974.

## Deputy members 2024–2026 (VERIFIED on stortinget.no case p=82361; no profile dirs created)
Geir Flikke (Høyre), Inger Skjelsbæk (SV) and Trine Skei Grande (Venstre). Party labels come from no.wikipedia. The terms run 1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2026. The earlier 2021–2023 deputies were Flikke, Skjelsbæk and Laila Dåvøy (KrF). nobelpeaceprize.org lists no deputies.
