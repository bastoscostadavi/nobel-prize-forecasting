My prediction: **Steven Berry, James Levinsohn, and Ariel Pakes**, for **developing methods to estimate demand and competition in markets with differentiated products**.

Their landmark contribution is the **Berry–Levinsohn–Pakes (BLP) framework**, which lets economists infer consumer preferences, firms’ markups, and competitive behavior from market data. It underpins much modern analysis of mergers, market power, and the welfare effects of changing prices or products.

I favor this trio because the contribution is foundational, widely applied, and clearly attributable to three researchers—a strong fit for a Nobel citation. This is a speculative forecast, with substantial uncertainty.
