## Biography

Born 1961 in Surahammar (per the Academy; "uppvuxen i Surahammar"); lives outside Stockholm. Studied political science, economics and literature. Worked at Dagens Nyheter 1990–2010 on literary and investigative reportage and on the editorial page. Guest professor of journalism at the University of Gothenburg 2012–13; honorary doctor of medicine, Uppsala University (2010). August Prize 2012 for her Wallenberg biography; H.M. the King's Medal 2020 "for significant contributions as an author." Has sat on the boards of Swedish PEN and the Publicistklubben. Elected to seat 5 in 2020, succeeding the sinologist Göran Malmqvist. Took office as Permanent Secretary on 1 June 2026, the second woman in the post after Sara Danius.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- **Children's and YA fiction:** *Jag heter Rosalie* (2001) and sequels; a YA novel about a bullied boy (2004).
- ***Pillret*** (2008): on depression, antidepressants, research and markets; Guldspaden journalism award.
- ***"Det står ett rum här och väntar på dig …" Berättelsen om Raoul Wallenberg*** (2012; English *Raoul Wallenberg: The Biography*, 2015), based on extensive archival research; August Prize.
- ***Nobel. Den gåtfulle Alfred, hans värld och hans pris*** (2019; English 2023): Nobel's life, his cosmopolitan world, his link to Bertha von Suttner, and the fight over his will.
- ***Marionetterna*** (2023): remote-controlled influence campaigns from interwar Berlin and Paris to today's troll factories.
- ***Den osynliga ledamoten*** (2026): on the poet Anna Maria Lenngren in Enlightenment Sweden.
- **Method:** she calls it "litterär sakprosa", combining academic precision with an investigative journalist's curiosity to build broad accounts of a period's mentality.

## Public statements and values

- On her writing (Fokus, date unverified): "Jag skulle aldrig hitta på att det regnade en dag 1840. Jag går tillbaka till väderobservationer för att se om det verkligen var så." The Academy matters "för att värna det svenska språket och litteraturen, i en tid där nätet till stor del utarmas av hatspråk."
- On becoming Permanent Secretary (SVT, Dec 2025): "Man måste skapa lite större lust kring det svenska språkets möjligheter"; concern about a reading crisis and the coarsening of language through polarisation.
- On AI (TT, May 2026, via search summary): human creativity must be defended; machines cannot understand literary expression.
- As Permanent Secretary she is expected to announce the 2026 laureate (TT via search summary).

## Connections

Married to former finance minister Pär Nuder (Wikipedia). Elected alongside Steve Sem-Sandberg in 2020. Her Nobel biography draws on Nobel Foundation and family archives. Predecessor Mats Malm; she works with chair Anders Olsson.
