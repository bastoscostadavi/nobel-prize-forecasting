# Attribution and scope review of the consolidated Medicine list

Reviewed against original papers, institutional scientific accounts and award organizations’ scientific histories, with the public-evidence cutoff fixed at 2 October 2026. Scientific basis statements summarize sources; inclusion, scope choices, reservations and recipient pools are editorial judgments. Award recognition helps locate evidence and is not proof of Nobel nomination or winning probability.

All discoveries from both drafts remain. Shared discoveries were merged by achievement rather than by exact wording or identical proposed recipients. The canonical JSON records the source entry IDs. No source-list frequency is used as a ranking signal.

## M001 — A bispecific antibody that substitutes for factor VIII activity in hemophilia A

**Credit pool:** Kunihiro Hattori; Takehisa Kitazawa; Tomoyuki Igawa.

**Input entries:** c:C001.

**Attribution judgment:** The 2026 Lasker supports this three-person pool; the citation should focus on factor-mimetic bispecific engineering rather than all antibody therapeutics.
