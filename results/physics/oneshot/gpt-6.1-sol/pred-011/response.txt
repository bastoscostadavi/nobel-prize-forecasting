My best guess is **a shared prize for Michael Berry and Yakir Aharonov**, for revealing how geometric and electromagnetic effects influence quantum phases.

Aharonov co-predicted the **Aharonov–Bohm effect**: electromagnetic potentials can affect a particle even where the electromagnetic field is zero. Berry identified the **geometric phase** acquired as a quantum system’s parameters trace a closed path. Both discoveries became foundational, with consequences across quantum physics and materials research.

They fit a familiar Nobel pattern: fundamental discoveries whose importance has been established over decades.
