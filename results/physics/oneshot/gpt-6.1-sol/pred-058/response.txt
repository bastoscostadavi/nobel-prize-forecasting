My speculative pick is **Yakir Aharonov and Michael Berry**, for discoveries revealing how phase and geometry shape quantum mechanics.

The **Aharonov–Bohm effect** shows how electromagnetic potentials can affect quantum particles even where the electromagnetic field vanishes. The **Berry phase** describes a geometric contribution to a quantum state’s evolution. Both discoveries are experimentally established and influential across modern physics—the kind of foundational work the Nobel committee often rewards decades later.
