# Attribution and merge review — independent Codex Chemistry draft

Prepared 3 October 2026. The scientific credit pools are starting points, not resolved Nobel recipient sets. No candidate probabilities, simulated nominations or actual nomination counts are included.

## Merge procedure

1. Compare discoveries by scientific scope, using `discovery_key` as a suggested key. IDs are local to this draft and must be reassigned after merging; do not join drafts by `candidate_id`.
2. Preserve a single entry for the same discovery while retaining supported alternative names and both sets of sources. Do not merge distinct discoveries merely because they share a scientist.
3. Keep base editing versus prime editing, fluorescent versus phosphorescent OLEDs, and first-principles dynamics versus metadynamics distinguishable. ATRP versus RAFT can be reviewed together but cannot automatically be compressed into one three-person award.
4. Choose a concrete discovery citation before selecting recipients. Three is a limit on the whole award, not on each discovery in a combined proposal. A pool of four or five names is not permission to propose four or five recipients.
5. Recheck living status, competing priority accounts and conflicts for the shortlist. Known deceased contributors are outside `credited_names` and must not re-enter the recipient pool during a union operation.
6. Carry forward `prior_award_flags`, `historical_contributors` and source provenance. Prior Nobel laureates can be considered for a distinct achievement; the same rewarded achievement should not be forecast again.
7. Cross-category discoveries may appear in Medicine and Chemistry inputs. This is intentional candidate coverage; it does not make two awards for the same work independent predictions.

## Particular attribution questions

- **Sequencing:** the Balasubramanian–Klenerman–Mayer trio is supported by the 2022 Breakthrough and 2026 Wolf accounts. Nanopore sequencing has a different and larger credit pool; neither should stand for every sequencing invention.
- **Precision genome editing:** Komor and Gaudelli should remain visible for the separate cytosine and adenine editor discoveries. Anzalone belongs to prime editing. Combining all three inventions forces an explicit credit choice.
- **Controlled polymerization:** distinguish Wang/Matyjaszewski copper work, the Sawamoto/Kamigaito lineage and the CSIRO RAFT team. Percec and complete original-author roles need deeper priority review if ATRP is shortlisted.
- **Single-atom catalysis:** do not infer that Clarivate's recognition of Zhang alone settles the roles of Liu, Wang, Qiao or other contributors in the original work.
- **Photoredox:** the 2021 organocatalysis prize does not automatically preclude MacMillan for a distinct invention. Nicewicz's role should not disappear when laboratory leaders are compared. Early photoredox precedents also matter.
- **PROTACs:** Sakamoto and Kim remain visible alongside Crews and Deshaies. A clinical-development citation could produce a different pool from the first conceptual experiment.
- **Proteomics:** an award for quantitative workflows differs from one for MudPIT or ionization. First authors and algorithm developers need contribution-level review.
- **RNA methylation:** chemical reversibility and transcriptome mapping are related but distinct achievements. Do not force the entire epitranscriptomics field into an unsupported three-name template.
- **DNA assembly:** Rothemund, Winfree and Shih are alternatives covering different DNA nanotechnology developments. Mirkin's DNA-particle assembly lineage should be compared with Alivisatos's independent work for a broad claim.
- **Lipid nanoparticles:** Cullis, MacLachlan, Hope and Madden are a provisional formulation pool. A thorough review must compare specific lipid inventions and competing industrial and academic claims.
- **Porous materials:** discrete cages, mesoporous silica, zeolites and COFs are chemically distinguishable from MOFs, but the 2025 prize changes the forecasting context. The COF entry is explicitly a boundary case.
- **Single-molecule methods:** chemical mechanistic discoveries, FRET distance measurement and optical trapping are distinct. A new chemical citation must avoid simply repeating Physics 2018 or Chemistry 2014.
- **Total synthesis:** retained for coverage, but a career-sized label needs narrowing to a defined strategy or coherent breakthrough. Target-specific priority could change the credit pool substantially.

## Retained boundary entries

These are not ranked low-probability entries. They are cases where independent significance or substantial prior-award overlap needs explicit examination before using the merged list in simulations.

- **Accurate exchange-correlation approximations for practical DFT** (`density-functional-approximations`): DFT itself won in 1998, and the pool spans different functional constructions.
- **Design of molecular-sieve catalysts with controlled pores and active sites** (`zeolite-catalyst-design`): An overly broad citation risks rewarding careers rather than a defined discovery.
- **Fast tetrazine ligation for molecular labeling** (`tetrazine-ligation`): Bioorthogonal chemistry was rewarded in 2022; another award needs a demonstrably separate advance.
- **Porous crystalline covalent organic frameworks** (`covalent-organic-frameworks`): Very close to the 2025 MOF prize and includes a 2025 laureate.
- **Single-molecule measurements of molecular motors and folding forces** (`single-molecule-mechanics`): The optical-tweezers invention was rewarded in Physics 2018.
- **Strategies for total synthesis of complex bioactive natural products** (`complex-natural-product-synthesis`): This is a broad program rather than a single invention; a Nobel citation should name a specific strategy or coherent set of breakthroughs.
- **Structural mechanisms of eukaryotic transcription machinery** (`transcription-machinery`): Transcription was already rewarded in Chemistry 2006 and cryo-EM in 2017.

## Exclusions and scope limits

Already rewarded achievements are not fresh standalone entries: metal-organic frameworks (2025), protein structure prediction and computational protein design (2024), quantum dots (2023), click and bioorthogonal chemistry as a whole (2022), asymmetric organocatalysis (2021), CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing itself (2020), lithium-ion batteries as a whole (2019), directed evolution and phage display as a whole (2018), cryo-EM as a method (2017), molecular machines as a whole (2016), super-resolution fluorescence microscopy as a whole (2014), multiscale chemical modeling as a whole (2013), carbon-carbon palladium cross-coupling as a whole (2010), ribosome structure (2009), and graphene isolation (Physics 2010). Related distinct advances remain with explicit flags.

Several broad career or application labels were not expanded into separate entries, including all nanocrystal chemistry, all drug-delivery technology, all green chemistry and all battery research. This prevents an arbitrary number of loosely defined candidates; it does not rule out adding a concrete, sourced discovery from these areas in the merge. The list is broad, not provably exhaustive.

No separate investigation of current Nobel nominations was attempted. Public precursor awards and publication influence are evidence of scientific recognition, not proof of nomination or a probability model.

## Committee and eligibility checks

The eight names in the local `agent-data/chemistry/committee/ROSTER.md` were compared with the provisional credit pools: no direct identity overlap was found. This relies on that roster and does not reverify committee membership. Coauthorship, mentoring and institutional relationships remain unreviewed.

Living status was checked selectively, particularly where historical pioneers could otherwise enter the award pool. Absence of an obituary is not proof of living status. Every proposed final recipient needs a current check before selection.

Nobel's official [Chemistry facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/facts-on-the-nobel-prize-in-chemistry/) support the three-person limit and the general exclusion of awards to people already deceased before the announcement. The already-awarded MOF discovery is documented in the [2025 official announcement](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2025/press-release/).

## Source quality

Original studies, researcher-authored scientific accounts, institutional reports and official award citations were preferred. Several direct pages returned 403, so their indexed source text was inspected. `accessed_on` records this research date, not a claim of successful full-text retrieval. One sponsored participant-interview feature supports the photoredox historical context alongside an original study; it should not settle priority. A full competing-priority audit is intentionally reserved for shortlisted discoveries.
