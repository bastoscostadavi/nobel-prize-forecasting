## Biography

Sartori earned a master's degree in physical chemistry at the University of Padova (1998) and a PhD in materials science and engineering at the University of Bologna (2003). She also holds a master's in science communication from ISAS/SISSA Trieste (2004). She was a research fellow at Padova (2000–2005) and then worked at the Institute for Energy Technology (IFE) in Norway, as a postdoc (2006–2010) and researcher (2010–2014). She moved to UNIK/UiO as associate professor in 2013 and later became full professor. Since 2018 she has led the Energy Systems section. Her honours include:
- the RSC Energy & Environmental Science Prize (2014);
- the IJHE David Sanborn Scott Award (2020);
- the UiO Education Award (2022);
- the UiO Titan science-communication prize (2023).

She served in the Materials Research Society presidential line (Vice President 2022, President 2023, Immediate Past President 2024).

## Research footprint

Her group synthesizes and characterizes materials for energy conversion and storage. Topics include:
- hydrogen storage and production;
- batteries;
- solar cells;
- wider energy systems.

A hallmark of the group is in-situ and operando structural characterization with synchrotron X-ray and powder neutron diffraction at large European and US facilities (UiO profile). Her 2019 hydrogen-storage paper was the most cited of its year in its IJHE category. She represents Norway in International Energy Agency Hydrogen tasks: Task 32 (2013–2018), Task 40 (2019–2024) and Task 51 on hydrogen materials for energy storage (2025–2027). Her focus has widened from metal-hydride materials science toward energy-system integration, innovation teaching and hydrogen technology.
