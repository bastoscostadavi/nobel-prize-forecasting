My prediction: **next-generation DNA sequencing**, awarded to:

- **Shankar Balasubramanian**
- **David Klenerman**
- **Pascal Mayer**

The citation would likely recognize **the invention and development of massively parallel DNA sequencing**, including methods for sequencing large numbers of DNA fragments simultaneously on a surface.

Balasubramanian and Klenerman developed the sequencing chemistry underlying Solexa, subsequently acquired by Illumina; Mayer pioneered DNA colony technology that helped establish massively parallel sequencing. Together, these advances dramatically reduced sequencing costs and transformed biological research.

This is a speculative pick, but its combination of chemical innovation and enormous scientific impact makes it my strongest candidate.
