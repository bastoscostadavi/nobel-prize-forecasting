## Biography

Peter Somfai was born in 1960 in Gothenburg. He received his MSc in chemistry and PhD in organic chemistry at Chalmers University of Technology, then was a postdoc with Satoru Masamune at MIT. He was appointed Professor of Organic Chemistry at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in 1999, was Head of the Department of Chemistry from 2001, and was Dean of the School of Chemical Science and Engineering from 2005 until 2007, when he stepped down to return to research. He was also Extraordinary Senior Researcher at the University of Tartu. In 2012 he moved to Lund University as Professor of Organic Chemistry. He was elected to the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences in 2010 and to the Royal Physiographic Society in Lund in 2015. He co-authored the textbook *Modern Organic Synthesis: An Introduction* (2nd ed., with Zweifel and Nantz).
