# Comparison of the two Medicine candidate lists

Comparison date: 2 October 2026. Claude has 40 discovery entries; the Codex draft has 32. Manual matching identifies 19 shared discovery families, 21 entries only in Claude and 13 only in Codex, producing a provisional union of 53. These are coverage counts, not rankings or forecast probabilities. Some matched families still have differences in citation scope.

The comparison combines Claude's `nominees` and `other_names` before comparing attribution pools. It normalizes Adrian Bird to Adrian P. Bird and Zhijian J. Chen to Zhijian James Chen. A name placed in `other_names` is retained rather than treated as absent. The machine-readable comparison records exact source-file hashes and every match.

Rosenberg's inclusion in the CAR-T pool, Lomo versus Collingridge in the LTP pool, and Sakurai's orexin contribution should be reviewed against the intended citation and original research. ## Correction to the Codex draft

Claude's method note correctly reported Habener as deceased. I initially included him based on older scientific award accounts and missed this eligibility fact. Version 2 removes him from the eligible pool, retains his historical credit, and regenerates the CSV and committee packet. The independent initial draft is preserved in `revisions/v1/`; no other Claude entries or names have been incorporated.

Habener died on 28 December 2025, as confirmed by the [Johns Hopkins institutional memorial](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/news/articles/2026/04/a-titan-in-endocrinology) and [Endocrine Society memorial](https://endocrinenews.endocrine.org/remembering-joel-habener-md/).

## Implications for committee coverage

Both lists contribute substantial additional coverage. A merged draft should preserve the provisional union and then resolve duplicate boundaries and attribution, rather than use agreement as an inclusion threshold. This comparison evaluates what the two files contain. It does not establish Nobel worthiness, current eligibility or scientific maturity for every entry unique to Claude; his file's living-status checks are self-reported and were not independently re-audited here. The Habener correction was independently verified.

No merged committee list or committee simulation has been created by this comparison.
