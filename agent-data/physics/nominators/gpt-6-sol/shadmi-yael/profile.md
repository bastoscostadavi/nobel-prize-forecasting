## Research footprint

Shadmi's early work was in supersymmetry phenomenology and dynamics. With Stephen Parke she studied spin correlations in top-quark pair production at e+e− colliders (1996). With Emanuel Katz and Yuri Shirman she studied slepton masses and the μ-term in anomaly-mediated supersymmetry breaking (1999). With Yuri Shirman she wrote the Reviews of Modern Physics article "Dynamical supersymmetry breaking" (2000). With Jonathan Feng and Yosef Nir she worked on flavour symmetries, neutrino parameters and lepton-flavour violation. Later work includes strongly interacting dark matter (with Boddy, Feng, Kaplinghat and Tait, 2014) and contributions to the 100 TeV pp collider BSM report (2016). Since 2018 her main programme has been building effective field theories with on-shell scattering-amplitude methods. This includes EFT amplitudes for scalar and vector couplings to gluons (2018), the electroweak EFT from on-shell amplitudes (2019), massive contact terms and "on-shell Higgsing" (2020–21), dimension-8 SMEFT (2022–24) and the "spurion massive EFT" (2026). She also proposes collider searches such as flavourful light scalars at FASER (2024).
