My prediction is **Elhanan Helpman, Gene M. Grossman, and Marc J. Melitz**.

The contribution: **explaining how differences among firms, innovation, and imperfect competition shape international trade and the gains from globalization.**

Helpman and Grossman developed influential theories connecting trade to multinational production, innovation, and growth. Melitz showed how opening to trade reallocates activity toward more productive firms, raising aggregate productivity while creating uneven gains across businesses.

Together, they offer a coherent prize recognizing the firm-level foundations of globalization. This is a speculative prediction; my confidence in the exact trio is low.
