My pick is **Michael Berry**, potentially sharing the prize with **Yakir Aharonov**, for discoveries concerning quantum phases.

Berry’s “geometric phase” explains how a quantum system can acquire a measurable phase through a cycle of changes. It has become fundamental to understanding quantum matter and optics. Aharonov’s work, particularly the Aharonov–Bohm effect, would fit a related shared award.

**Confidence: low.** Nobel timing is exceptionally hard to predict: the committee could recognize any of several decades-old breakthroughs. But Berry would be my single-name bet.
