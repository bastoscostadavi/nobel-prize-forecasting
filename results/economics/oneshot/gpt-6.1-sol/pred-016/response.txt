My prediction is **Elhanan Helpman, Gene Grossman, and Marc Melitz**, for explaining **how firms, market structure, and innovation shape international trade**.

A plausible prize citation:

> “For advancing the theory of international trade through the study of imperfect competition, innovation, and heterogeneous firms.”

Helpman and Grossman developed influential theories connecting trade to innovation, growth, and government policy. Melitz showed how differences in firms’ productivity determine which firms export and how opening to trade reallocates production, raising aggregate productivity.
