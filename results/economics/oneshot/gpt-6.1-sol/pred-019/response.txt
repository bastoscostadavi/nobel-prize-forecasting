My prediction is **Elhanan Helpman, Gene M. Grossman, and Marc J. Melitz**, for **explaining how firms, market structure, and the organization of production shape international trade**.

- **Helpman:** showed how economies of scale and imperfect competition explain trade patterns and multinational activity.
- **Grossman:** developed theories of trade policy, outsourcing, and the international division of production.
- **Melitz:** showed how trade reallocates activity toward more productive firms, raising aggregate productivity while creating uneven gains and losses.

The strongest case is that their work connects globalization to decisions by individual firms—who exports, who expands, and where production happens—with major implications for trade policy.

This is a speculative forecast; my confidence in this exact trio is low.
