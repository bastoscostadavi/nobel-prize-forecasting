My pick is **Charles L. Kane, Eugene J. Mele, and Laurens W. Molenkamp**, for their work on **topological insulators and the quantum spin Hall effect**—materials that can insulate internally while conducting along their edges.

That work fits the Nobel pattern: a fundamental theoretical advance, convincing experimental confirmation, and enough time for its importance to become established. It would also build on the broader recognition of topology in the 2016 physics prize.

My runner-up would be **Pablo Jarillo-Herrero, Allan MacDonald, and Rafi Bistritzer**, for magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene.

I’d have low confidence in any exact prediction, but Kane–Mele–Molenkamp is the trio I’d choose.
