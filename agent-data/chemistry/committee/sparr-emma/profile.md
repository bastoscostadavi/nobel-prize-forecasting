## Biography

Emma Sparr received her PhD in physical chemistry from Lund University in 2001. She was a postdoc at the Department of Pharmacy, Uppsala University, and in the Biochemistry of Membranes group at Utrecht University, then returned to Lund in 2004 to start her own group; she is now Professor in the Division of Physical Chemistry. She directs the COMMONS Center of Excellence, has chaired the Swedish Research Council's council for natural and engineering sciences, and holds faculty and institutional leadership roles at Lund. Honours (per search summaries of her Lund page): Hagberg Foundation Prize (KVA, 2014), Arrhenius Medal of the Swedish Chemical Society (2019), membership of the Royal Swedish Academy of Engineering Sciences (2020). She was elected to the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, Class for chemistry, in January 2026.

## Research / intellectual footprint

- Lipid self-assembly and phase behaviour, including non-equilibrium conditions (e.g. lipids reorganising at drying interfaces).
- Biological membranes as physical-chemical systems: the skin as a barrier to diffusion; how small polar molecules (osmolytes) protect membranes against dehydration and osmotic stress; lung surfactant.
- Lipid-protein co-assembly in amyloid formation (links to Alzheimer's and Parkinson's research), supported by Wallenberg funding ("the role of fats in protein clumps").
- Membrane remodelling.
