My prediction is **next-generation DNA sequencing**, particularly the invention of massively parallel sequencing methods that made sequencing whole genomes fast and affordable.

The three laureates I would pick are:

- **Shankar Balasubramanian** — co-developed sequencing by synthesis.
- **David Klenerman** — co-developed the technology with Balasubramanian.
- **Pascal Mayer** — pioneered massively parallel sequencing using arrays of DNA colonies.

These methods transformed genetics, medicine, and biological research, and their impact has had time to become firmly established—a strong profile for a Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

This is a speculative forecast, not knowledge of the committee’s decision.
