My prediction is **Steven Berry, James Levinsohn, and Ariel Pakes**, for **developing empirical methods to study differentiated-product markets and market power**.

Their landmark “BLP” framework lets economists estimate how consumers choose among competing products, then assess pricing, competition, and mergers. It has become foundational to empirical industrial economics and influential in antitrust analysis.

This would recognize a clear, shared methodological breakthrough with substantial practical impact—a strong fit for the prize. **Confidence: low**, because Nobel timing and the committee’s choice of field are highly unpredictable.
