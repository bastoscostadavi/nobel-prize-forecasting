My pick is **Charles Kane and Eugene Mele, possibly sharing with Laurens Molenkamp, for topological insulators**.

Kane and Mele developed foundational theory for materials that insulate in their interior but conduct along their edges or surfaces, with that conduction protected by topology. Molenkamp led landmark experimental work demonstrating the quantum spin Hall effect. That combination of deep theory and convincing experiments makes a strong Nobel case.

The 2016 Physics Nobel recognized related discoveries in topology, which could either strengthen this case or make the committee favor another field.
